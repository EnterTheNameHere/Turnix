===============================================================================
DESIGN AREA DA-03 - TRACE RECORDS
===============================================================================

Status:
-------
EVOLVING

Description:
------------
Defines the implementation-independent portable evidence model emitted by the
Actant tracing substrate.

Purpose:
--------
This document defines trace-record structure and invariants independently of
any implementation language, storage codec, transport, viewer, or debugger.

It defines:
- trace record identity and producer ordering
- record kinds
- event, span-start, and span-end structure
- trace type definitions
- explicit causal and logical references
- span structural references
- runtime correlations
- exception evidence
- timestamp semantics
- record immutability and portability


===============================================================================
01. RECORD MODEL
===============================================================================

01.01. A TraceRecord is one immutable unit of trace evidence after creation.

01.02. TraceRecord is the fundamental evidence unit of the tracing substrate.

01.03. A TraceRecord does not contain or require a materialized trace DAG.

01.04. A TraceRecord contains enough explicit identity and relationship
      information for consumers to reconstruct supported relationships when
      the referenced evidence is available.

01.05. TraceRecord semantics are independent of concrete in-memory object
      representation and serialization format.


===============================================================================
02. COMMON RECORD FIELDS
===============================================================================

02.01. Every TraceRecord contains:
      - eventId
      - traceProducerId
      - sequence
      - timestampUnixNs
      - timestampMonotonicNs
      - kind
      - domain
      - type
      - traceTypeDefinitionId
      - level
      - message
      - attributes
      - causedBy

02.02. A TraceRecord may additionally contain, according to record kind and
      available context:
      - exceptionSnapshot
      - spanId
      - spanStartEventId
      - parentSpanId
      - spanFamily
      - origin
      - outcome
      - durationNs
      - Actant runtime correlation identifiers

02.03. Field names in this document identify platform concepts. Concrete
      language bindings may adapt surface syntax without changing meaning.


===============================================================================
03. RECORD IDENTITY AND PRODUCER ORDER
===============================================================================

03.01. eventId uniquely identifies one TraceRecord within the trace evidence
      identity domain.

03.02. traceProducerId identifies the producer that created the record.

03.03. sequence is a producer-local ordering value.

03.04. For one producer, successfully created records have unique sequence
      values in creation order.

03.05. Sequence values from different producers are not directly comparable
      as one global sequence.

03.06. Consumers do not infer cross-producer causality from sequence values.

03.07. Producer identity plus sequence supports auditing producer-local record
      continuity when the relevant records are available.

03.08. Missing producer-local sequence values in retained evidence may indicate
      missing evidence, filtering, partial selection, or another documented
      boundary. The gap alone does not identify its cause.


===============================================================================
04. TIMESTAMPS
===============================================================================

04.01. timestampUnixNs records an observed wall-clock emission time.

04.02. timestampMonotonicNs records producer-local monotonic timing evidence.

04.03. Timestamps are observational evidence.

04.04. Timestamps do not define causal relationships.

04.05. A consumer does not fabricate causal edges from timestamp order.

04.06. Monotonic timestamps from different clock domains are not assumed to
      be directly comparable.

04.07. durationNs may be derived from a compatible monotonic clock domain for
      a span whose implementation can measure such duration.


===============================================================================
05. RECORD KINDS
===============================================================================

05.01. The tracing substrate defines three structural record kinds:
      - event
      - spanStart
      - spanEnd

05.02. An event represents an occurrence that does not itself establish or
      terminate a span lifecycle.

05.03. A spanStart record establishes a span.

05.04. A spanEnd record terminates a normally terminally-recorded span.

05.05. Domain semantics are represented by trace types rather than by
      proliferating structural record kinds.


===============================================================================
06. EVENT RECORDS
===============================================================================

06.01. An event is either parentless structural evidence or evidence contained
      by a span.

06.02. A parentless event has no spanId and no spanStartEventId.

06.03. A parentless event records an origin describing its independent trace
      origin category.

06.04. A span-contained event records spanId and spanStartEventId.

06.05. A span-contained event does not claim an independent origin.

06.06. Structural containment does not prevent an event from also carrying
      explicit causedBy references.


===============================================================================
07. SPAN-START RECORDS
===============================================================================

07.01. A spanStart record contains spanId.

07.02. For a spanStart record, spanStartEventId identifies that same start
      record.

07.03. A spanStart record identifies its spanFamily.

07.04. A root span start has no parentSpanId and records an origin.

07.05. A child span start records parentSpanId and does not claim an
      independent origin.

07.06. parentSpanId expresses structural span nesting.

07.07. Structural nesting is distinct from arbitrary causal or logical
      references.


===============================================================================
08. SPAN-END RECORDS
===============================================================================

08.01. A spanEnd record identifies spanId.

08.02. A spanEnd record identifies the corresponding spanStartEventId.

08.03. A spanEnd record identifies spanFamily.

08.04. A spanEnd record records an outcome.

08.05. A spanEnd record may record durationNs.

08.06. A spanEnd record does not establish a new parent span or independent
      origin.

08.07. A spanEnd record does not exist as valid complete span evidence without
      a corresponding span start in the evidence universe from which the
      record originated.

08.08. A partial retained or exported evidence set may contain a spanEnd whose
      start is unavailable only when the evidence set represents that
      incompleteness honestly.

08.09. Tooling distinguishes invalid evidence from valid evidence whose
      dependencies are absent from the currently available evidence set.


===============================================================================
09. TRACE TYPE DEFINITIONS
===============================================================================

09.01. traceTypeDefinitionId identifies the semantic TraceTypeDefinition used
      to interpret the record.

09.02. A TraceTypeDefinition is immutable semantic metadata.

09.03. A TraceTypeDefinition is either an event definition or a span-family
      definition.

09.04. An event definition identifies the domain and event semantic name.

09.05. A span-family definition identifies:
      - domain
      - span family name
      - generated start type
      - generated terminal outcome types

09.06. A generated type defines at least its semantic label, presentation
      level, and display name.

09.07. Trace type definitions have a schema version.

09.08. TraceTypeDefinition identity is content-addressed from canonical
      semantic definition content.

09.09. Concrete canonical encoding and digest algorithm belong to an
      interoperability or implementation specification unless standardized
      across all Actant implementations.

09.10. A consumer interprets a record using the exact definition identified
       by traceTypeDefinitionId rather than guessing semantics from display
       text.


===============================================================================
10. DOMAIN, TYPE, LEVEL, MESSAGE, AND ATTRIBUTES
===============================================================================

10.01. domain identifies the semantic subsystem or subject area of the trace
       type.

10.02. type identifies the semantic generated or event type represented by
       the record.

10.03. level classifies presentation severity.

10.04. level does not by itself define runtime correctness or authority.

10.05. message provides human-readable evidence presentation.

10.06. Machine interpretation does not rely on message text when structured
       semantic fields provide the meaning.

10.07. attributes carries structured event-specific evidence.

10.08. The owning domain defines the meaning and required presence of its
       domain-specific attributes.

10.09. Attributes may contain stable Actant identifiers when the emitting
       domain owns or knows those identifiers.

10.10. Attribute representation remains portable and immutable after record
       creation.


===============================================================================
11. EXPLICIT REFERENCES
===============================================================================

11.01. causedBy contains explicit creator-asserted causal or logical
       TraceReferences.

11.02. A TraceReference contains:
      - kind
      - id

11.03. kind identifies the semantic identity category of the referenced
      target.

11.04. id is a stable exact identifier meaningful for that reference kind.

11.05. Reference identifiers are not restricted to one universal identifier
      encoding.

11.06. A record does not contain the same normalized TraceReference more than
      once in causedBy.

11.07. Reference order is preserved as emitted but does not imply causal
      precedence unless the owning semantic definition explicitly gives that
      order meaning.

11.08. causedBy is explicit evidence. A consumer does not add causedBy
      references merely because records occurred near each other in time.

11.09. causedBy may reference evidence or domain identities that are not
      present in the consumer's currently available evidence set.

11.10. An unresolved reference is represented as unresolved rather than
       silently discarded or fabricated.


===============================================================================
12. STRUCTURAL RELATIONSHIPS AND CAUSAL REFERENCES
===============================================================================

12.01. Span structural fields and causedBy serve different purposes.

12.02. spanId and spanStartEventId identify span membership and span lifecycle
       structure.

12.03. parentSpanId identifies structural nesting between spans.

12.04. causedBy records explicit causal or logical relationships not reduced
       to structural span nesting.

12.05. Tooling may project both structural and causal relationships into a
       full trace graph while preserving their distinct edge meanings.

12.06. The legacy generic parentEventIds field is not part of this record
       model.


===============================================================================
13. ORIGIN
===============================================================================

13.01. origin identifies why a record or root span begins without structural
       containment by another span in the same producer context.

13.02. Origin is structural evidence, not a substitute for a domain trace
       type.

13.03. A record that is structurally contained by a span does not also claim
       an independent origin.

13.04. Concrete origin categories are defined by the tracing substrate or the
       owning domain as appropriate.


===============================================================================
14. OUTCOMES
===============================================================================

14.01. outcome identifies the terminal semantic category of a spanEnd record.

14.02. Standard span outcome categories include:
      - completed
      - failed
      - errored
      - cancelled

14.03. A span family may define additional generated outcomes.

14.04. Outcome identity is machine-readable and does not depend on message
      wording.

14.05. Absence of a spanEnd record from an available evidence set does not by
      itself prove which terminal outcome occurred.


===============================================================================
15. RUNTIME CORRELATION
===============================================================================

15.01. TraceRecord may carry identifiers that correlate the record with
      Actant runtime identity domains.

15.02. The current tracing contract includes correlation positions for:
      - Actant run identity
      - Application identity
      - ApplicationRun identity

15.03. Correlation fields are optional when the corresponding runtime identity
      is not known or not applicable at emission time.

15.04. Correlation is evidence linkage. It does not grant authority over the
      correlated object.

15.05. Additional correlation identities may be added through explicit design
      evolution without changing the distinction between correlation and
      authority.


===============================================================================
16. EXCEPTION EVIDENCE
===============================================================================

16.01. A TraceRecord may carry an ExceptionSnapshot when an exception or
      equivalent implementation-language failure object contributes evidence
      to the record.

16.02. ExceptionSnapshot is immutable captured evidence rather than a live
      implementation exception object.

16.03. Exception evidence preserves diagnostic information without requiring
      the original exception object to remain alive.

16.04. Exception evidence does not acquire committed-state authority.

16.05. Language-specific exception introspection and capture mechanics belong
      to implementation specifications.

16.06. A cross-language representation preserves semantic diagnostic content
      without requiring identical host-language exception models.


===============================================================================
17. PORTABILITY AND IMMUTABILITY
===============================================================================

17.01. Trace records and trace type definitions are designed to cross process,
      language, storage, and tooling boundaries without changing semantic
      meaning.

17.02. Once a TraceRecord is created, its evidence fields do not change.

17.03. Once a TraceTypeDefinition identity is established, the semantic
      content associated with that identity does not change.

17.04. Mutable implementation objects are normalized or copied as necessary
      before becoming retained TraceRecord evidence.

17.05. A consumer does not mutate received evidence in place and then present
      the result as the original record.

17.06. Derived annotations belong to tooling state or derived artifacts and
      remain distinguishable from producer-emitted evidence.


===============================================================================
18. LOCKED MIGRATION DECISIONS
===============================================================================

18.01. LOCKED:
      - TraceRecord is the fundamental emitted evidence unit
      - records are immutable after creation
      - producer identity and producer-local sequence are explicit
      - timestamps are observational and do not define causality
      - event, spanStart, and spanEnd are structural record kinds
      - trace type definitions carry machine-readable semantic identity
      - span structure uses spanId, spanStartEventId, and parentSpanId
      - causedBy carries explicit creator-asserted causal or logical links
      - structural span relationships and causedBy relationships remain
        semantically distinct
      - the record model does not use generic parentEventIds
      - the record model does not require a generic reason field
      - domain meaning is carried by trace types, outcomes, attributes,
        correlations, and references
      - records may be correlated with Actant runtime identities
      - exception evidence is captured as portable evidence rather than kept
        as a live host-language exception object
      - graph materialization is a tooling concern
