===============================================================================
ACTANT DESIGN AREA - FLOW AND CONTRACTUAL ORCHESTRATION
===============================================================================

Status:
-------
EVOLVING

Description:
------------
Defines the conceptual responsibilities and continuity guarantees of Flow.

Purpose:
--------
Explains contract-governed communication, evidence, identity, recovery,
and boundaries between Flow, domains, and Actant infrastructure.

It does NOT define concrete protocols, storage, or implementation APIs.


===============================================================================
01. PURPOSE AND RESPONSIBILITY
===============================================================================

01.01. Flow coordinates contractual communication and progression
       between domain participants across execution and availability changes.
01.02. Domains own intention, processing meaning, business success,
       independent Jobs, and domain effects. Flow does not interpret them.
01.03. Flow owns relationship knowledge, Messages, attempts, acknowledgements,
       available Results, contracts, progression, and unmet obligations.
01.04. Actant provides persistence, lifecycle, execution, routing,
       Transport, resource authority, recovery, and coordination facilities.
01.05. Flow includes Invocation, Subscription, and Emit semantics.
       It is not a universal domain workflow engine.


===============================================================================
02. CONTRACTS AND EVIDENCE
===============================================================================

02.01. Contracts determine the evidence and guarantees required for
       admission, delivery, processing, confirmation, and completion.
02.02. Transport-level receipt is not Flow admission. Flow recognition
       is not callback execution or source-domain success.
02.03. A callback returning a value, null, or error is an observed
       outcome; it is not automatically domain confirmation or commit.
02.04. Domain confirmations express domain-asserted facts. Flow verifies
       whether agreed evidence exists; it does not create domain truth.
02.05. A later causally sufficient Message may acknowledge an earlier
       obligation without requiring an additional standalone ACK.
02.06. One communication may satisfy several obligations but need not
       satisfy all outstanding obligations.
02.07. A valid Result and failure of a stronger confirmation or
       durability promise may coexist and must be reported truthfully.


===============================================================================
03. IDENTITY AND RELATIONSHIPS
===============================================================================

03.01. Relationship and operation identities are logical identities,
       distinct from Transports, attempts, payload equality, and timestamps.
03.02. Identities remain unambiguous within the promised recognition
       scope and survive runtime restoration where contracts require.
03.03. Quiescence, Unexpected Unavailability, and Termination describe
       different situations; temporary absence does not equal termination.
03.04. A relationship retains its identity through quiescence, unexpected
       unavailability, Transport replacement, and interrupted termination.
03.05. Completed termination ends that relationship. A newly established
       relationship has a new identity and does not automatically inherit
       earlier Invocation or Result history.
03.06. Independent Jobs may outlive their original relationship without
       requiring every later relationship to expose their history.
03.07. Listener-specific obligations remain independent; one unavailable
       listener need not stop another listener or unrelated domain work.


===============================================================================
04. DURABILITY BEFORE EFFECTS
===============================================================================

04.01. For Actant-managed potentially effectful actions, establish
       sufficient durable intent or admission before crossing the
       effect boundary, according to the applicable contract.
04.02. Record observed outcomes and the evidence needed before further
       externally observable actions relying on those outcomes.
04.03. An outgoing send attempt requires sufficient registered intent;
       recording a send attempt does not prove Flow reception.
04.04. An effectful callback requires durable admission before entry.
       Failure to establish required admission forbids callback execution.
04.05. Durable admission followed by interruption without completion
       evidence makes callback outcome uncertain. Do not blindly rerun it.
04.06. Retry of the same logical Message is not permission to execute
       an effectful callback again. Deduplication must be supported by
       stable identity and adequate receiver evidence.
04.07. A new independently identified Job requires separate authority
       and may duplicate an unresolved earlier external effect.


===============================================================================
05. RESTORATION AND RECONCILIATION
===============================================================================

05.01. On recovery, restore last durably known semantic state first;
       reconcile it with present participant and external evidence next.
05.02. Restore identities, contracts, outstanding Messages, confirmations,
       callback admission and outcomes, deadlines, and obligations as
       required. Do not treat restoration as runtime memory cloning.
05.03. Different participants may hold different valid partial knowledge.
       Reconcile unsettled obligations using sufficiently scoped evidence.
05.04. Missing historical information or NotFound is not proof that
       an action never occurred, failed, or may safely be executed again.
05.05. Contradictory authoritative outcomes for the same operation
       indicate a broken guarantee or evidence problem, not merely
       ordinary asymmetric knowledge. Contain and diagnose truthfully.
05.06. Retain sufficient evidence for promised recovery, deduplication,
       and delivery; complete permanent Message history is not required.
05.07. A caller-visible Flow error never ends Actant's responsibility
       for remaining reconciliation, recovery, and safe cleanup.


===============================================================================
06. PERSISTENCE DEGRADATION
===============================================================================

06.01. Known durability degradation is first-class information. Relevant
       Flow consumers can learn that a required promise is unavailable
       before attempting an action that depends on it.
06.02. Persistence failure does not automatically mean callback failure,
       relationship termination, or invalidation of available Results.
06.03. A Result may be available in runtime memory even when its intended
       durability could not be established. Report both facts accurately.
06.04. Delivery under degraded conditions follows the actual contract.
       Do not send when durable-before-send is a required precondition.
06.05. Flow may continue independent safe work and communicate failures
       without falsely claiming unavailable durability guarantees.
06.06. Later restoration of storage does not retroactively establish
       durability that was missing at an earlier boundary.


===============================================================================
07. IMPLEMENTATION AND TEST BOUNDARIES
===============================================================================

07.01. Contract profiles, retry and retention horizons, scheduling,
       storage representation, and resource arbitration require
       their own design decisions and implementation evidence.
07.02. Flow and MemoryValueSystem have related persistence concerns but
       no mandatory shared ledger, co-commit, or universal root design.
07.03. Architectural Tests cover admission-before-effect, crash
       uncertainty, deduplication, evidence contradictions, and recovery.
07.04. Favor simple correct behavior and truthful unresolved outcomes
       before speculative mechanisms or optimization.
07.05. This document does not specify a Transport protocol, callback API,
       contract DSL, scheduler, ledger layout, or storage technology.
