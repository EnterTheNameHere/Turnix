Current entry path
------------------

loads config and the manual activation plan, creates or loads a durable
Application through RuntimeHost, and invokes either:
- evilAnalysis.previewPrompt@1
- evilAnalysis.run@1

When a Job reports authoritativeStateAccepted, the CLI persists the Application
through runtime.saveApplication(). Prompt preview writes its projection only
after the preview Job has returned.
