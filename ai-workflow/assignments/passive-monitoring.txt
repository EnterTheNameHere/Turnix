COMMON CONTRACT
---------------
Applications request needed observations through a provider-neutral authorized
facade. They do not select hardware vendors, construct telemetry collectors or
manage sharing. Sessions outliving an invocation require independent valid owner
authority and must not retain an expired CodeEntryContext. Explicit release and
owner teardown must both revoke that session's collection claim.

Observations are optional, non-authoritative and disposable. Bound retained data;
represent unavailable hardware explicitly. Backend/Pack placement is replaceable.
Do not create automatic Pack activation authority as a shortcut. Reuse already
available providers or report unavailability until activation policy is established.

Host-process monotonic time governs rates/alignment; wall time is display evidence.
Sampling takes time: represent its interval/gaps rather than claiming simultaneous
hardware reads. Cross-process/host clock correlation is excluded.

Do not add automatic optimization, model/server restarts, tuning, speculative/MTP
experiments, long-term persistence, graphical UI, resource policy or a scheduler.
Do not modify consumer application branches. Engine-native metrics remain optional
and engine-owned; character counts must never be relabeled as model tokens.
