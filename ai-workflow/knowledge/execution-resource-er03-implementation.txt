Lifecycle and rollback boundary
--------------------------------
New providerComposition.py supplies a generic trusted composer/snapshot and issued
activation attempt. Lifecycle prepare stages the FULL plan/owners/registrations;
composition runs outside host/runtime/registry/broker/domain lanes; resume validates
the same attempt before hooks/creation commit/save/start/public host registration.
Existing no-composer lifecycle order and public create/load signatures remain.
Lifecycle Contexts acquire no direct LLM execution; hooks call normal capabilities.
Ordinary capability/Job execution keeps the accepted runtime serialization lane.

Private pending initializations reserve load identity and are not publicly looked
up. Invocation/reentrant activation cannot enter prepare/compose. Snapshot grants
are fixed; later owners/reactivated incarnations get no implicit grant or live
extension. Direct PackLoader batches use the same barrier and reverse only new
Packs/owners/scopes/modules/bindings on later failure, preserving previous work.

Host/runtime/application close checks BOTH pending initializations and later
activation attempts in active runtimes before any new finalizer/legacy teardown.
Admission is revoked callback-free; an outstanding composer returns typed pending
cleanup, never waits across a lane or loses its exact objects. A late callback
cannot revive the attempt. Outermost lifetime cleanup also honors this gate.
After unwind, explicit retry can drain known cleanup; unknown remains reported.
Published binding lifetime transfers only at accepted R1 publication. Failure
unwinds all new scopes/owners/modules and reports primary plus cleanup disposition.
Composition and new rollback/lease stop/wait callbacks run outside enclosing lanes;
legacy arbitrary Pack callbacks are not claimed globally outside serialization.
