03. BINDING SHARED CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------
A. Freeze the admitted process request BEFORE execution. The App requires ACK of
the exact resolved argv/cwd/tool/policy intent before any spawn. A result available
only after run() returns cannot supply that barrier. Propose one concrete minimal
prepare/execute or pre-launch acknowledgement mechanism. Bind execution to that
request, Context/Job and one-use admission; acknowledgement failure/cancellation
prevents launch. Do not call archive/client callbacks under runtime/control locks.
No filesystem observation/hash attests which executable image was actually loaded.

B. Specify a finite deadline and per-stream raw byte limits enforced while reading,
before buffer growth, with concurrent stdout/stderr handling and bounded drain.
Separate retained bytes, bytes observed and unknown totals; never call a truncated
stream complete. Decode only after bounded raw capture with explicit replacement
facts. Overflow, timeout and cancellation stop later App steps and require owned
disposition; no automatic retry of any possibly started process.

C. Specify cancellation admission and spawn/register races, callback withdrawal,
normal exit racing stop, first BaseException and cleanup failures, partial output,
and launch-attempted versus started evidence. Forward actual Job cancellation;
do not hold the ApplicationRun lane to request stop from another thread. Callbacks
must be idempotent/no-fail and avoid blocking pipe close/reader ownership races.
Preserve a cancellation primary as cancellation through existing Job semantics.

D. A claimed confirmed boundary needs owned child reaped and owned readers/drain
resolved. Scope this precisely; EOF/exit is not universal process-tree quiescence.
Descendant-held pipes and unknown cleanup stop the suite, not silently release a
workspace still in use or allow another unit to proceed. Specify a bounded failure
disposition when cleanup cannot finish: who retains exact owned resources and what
Context invalidation may do. Do not promise both unconditional finite cleanup and
guaranteed arbitrary descendant termination. No process-name kill, borrowed child,
global recovery or force-clearing UNKNOWN.

E. Generic error/trace text must omit raw arguments, output and private paths,
including timeout exceptions and any automatically surfaced cause/context text.
Private immutable structured evidence is separately available to the App archive;
safe errors must not destroy it. Test actual capability failure tracing, not just
the wrapper error's type. Avoid a broad tracing subsystem change for this scope.

F. Workspace read contract must specify exact return type/fields, byte cap enforced
by maxBytes+1 read, regular-file/containment/reparse handling, opened-file identity,
observed length/hash and invalidation/failure behavior. Reject detected changes;
metadata comparison is not proof against hostile same-size in-place mutation.
Clarify whether identity/hash are returned or derived from the returned bounded
bytes, rather than implying bare readBytes returns unstated metadata. No raw App
filesystem bypass or generic persistent workspace manager.

Existing callers and normal nonzero exit semantics stay compatible. Proposed new
bounded API is reusable platform code, not an App-only wrapper. Exact signatures,
fields/enums, errors and cleanup semantics require the next shared review.
