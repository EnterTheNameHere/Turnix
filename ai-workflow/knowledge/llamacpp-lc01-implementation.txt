Evidence and review details
---------------------------
Private immutable evidence separates requested/provenance/omission, input roles,
planned argv, actual ordered argv after successful Popen, launch generation,
directory, relevant environment and build snapshot, and unknown/unqueried provider
effective behavior. Target/mmproj/template paths identify inputs; their content
hashes remain null. Executable observed SHA-256/stat is separate from configured
path, pinned source and assignment user-reported build context. A later probe
does not overwrite prior launch build evidence or returned snapshots. profileId
is a configuration/input identity, not a model content hash or behavior identity.
Full evidence may contain caller-supplied credentials and is explicitly private;
no indiscriminate logging/telemetry/G-01 export is added. Automatic launchProfile
is deliberately narrower and marked requested-not-effective.

Working directory is captured alongside environment for identity and Popen, so
relative paths cannot resolve against a changed ambient directory. Compatibility
is conservative: different directories can conflict even for absolute inputs.
External mode rejects all non-null new fields at either scope, even false/zero/
empty kwargs, and cannot require/certify a remote build by local probing. Legacy
external startup-shaped settings remain readable but unapplied, with no managed
actual/planned argv. No remote effective settings are invented.

capabilityCheck='none' preserves lazy unprobed/fake/minimal profiles; unchecked
settings stay unverified. 'required' lazily checks reported pinned prefix and
advertised emitted/inherited controls before first launch. Success is explicitly
advertised-pinned-surface, not effective support or build-source attestation.
Each help/version child has a 5-second deadline and retains at most 256 KiB;
temporary capture is polled every 10 ms and can overshoot before kill. Children
are reaped; cache is bounded to 16 observed executable identities. Probe environment
strips LLAMA_ARG_* and passes no model arguments. Version text alone, missing
binary/flags, wrong identity, malformed help, timeout/nonzero/overflow do not become
positive support. Unknown extraArgs remain uncertified after successful probing.
