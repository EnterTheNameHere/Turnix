Corrected protocol
--------------------
Only backend/llm/executionSessions.py (version 2), streamingRuntime.py (version 13)
and new tests/backend/llm/test_executionBindingPublication.py (version 1) change.
Bind installs an exact-registration pending ticket before callback construction.
Host, registry, owner and provider-registration revocation invalidate pending
tickets. Exact registration tombstones also cover revocation before any binding
exists. Old owner/registration incarnations never authorize new admission.
An LlmProviderRegistry.executionPublication guard shares the actual registration
publication/withdrawal lane. Final commit holds registry then broker lanes, checks
exact registration identity, exact live ticket, admission and live owner grants.
Construction, participant cleanup and provider/lease callbacks run outside both
lanes. RevokeAll releases its broker lane before acquiring registry lanes.

Assignment into the published binding table is the lease-transfer point. Before
that point the composition caller owns its lease, including failed bind. Every
issued unpublished participant is disposed on rejection or partial construction;
remaining disposals are attempted if one close fails, preserving the primary error.
Failed participant rollback is retained/reported by finishClose as typed cleanup
failure, with no hidden retry/recovery and no takeover of the caller's lease.
Pending construction likewise prevents finishClose from falsely reporting drained.
After publication the normal binding lifetime owns/finalizes the lease exactly once.
A post-commit exception is marked llmBindingPublished=True; published participants
are not rolled back as unpublished and normal revocation still disposes them.
Rollback failure marks the primary error with safe llmPublicationCleanup detail.
Controlled binding intent remains fail-closed during/after rejected publication;
it cannot silently fall back to legacy execution. Exact replacement registration
requires explicit trusted rebinding; a new live owner incarnation can be granted.
