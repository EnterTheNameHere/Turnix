Correction 1: keep independent reasoning controls independent
------------------------------------------------------------
Remove proposal precedence rule 4's rejection of thinking-off combined with effort,
positive budget or budget message. Pinned common/arg.cpp around 3685-3728 assigns
mode, template effort and sampling budget/message separately; it does not establish
that these combinations are invalid. Startup values may also be defaults for later
request overrides. Accept and preserve these independently valid combinations,
without claiming dormant fields took effect while thinking was off.
Continue rejecting true duplicate authority for the same setting, including the
dedicated field and its corresponding template key, even if values happen to match.
Do not invent model/template compatibility restrictions. Test off+effort,
off+positive budget/message and the distinction between formatting and generation.

Correction 2: bind relevant environment inputs to the shared lease identity
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Launch-time capture plus optional explicit drift checking is insufficient: another
ApplicationRun can otherwise lease the same raw-config key under different inherited
startup inputs. Before managed lease acquisition, resolve/freeze the pinned relevant
owned LLAMA_ARG_* inputs (including absence) alongside the validated config. Use
the same frozen snapshot for the compatibility key and driver construction.
For Popen, retain ordinary non-owned environment inheritance but explicitly apply
the frozen owned values/absences, so later ambient mutations cannot silently change
this driver's startup settings. Record the actual relevant launch snapshot privately.
All collision checks must include inherited aliases/template kwargs that set an
owned value; inspect pinned spellings rather than relying on a guessed name list.

A newly acquired lease under changed relevant inputs must follow existing typed
SharedServiceConflictError behavior rather than share the old driver silently.
An existing lease keeps its frozen startup inputs; ambient changes alone do not
terminate its resident process. Preserve lazy startup and final-lease teardown.
This is provider-local input resolution, not a new backend arbitration policy.
Test two ApplicationRuns with equal config/different relevant environment, mutation
between acquire and launch, unchanged-env sharing, absence versus value, conflicts,
and no additional server/restart/lease leaks. Full speculation inputs remain LC-03.
