Finding ER-02-R1: publication can miss a winning revocation
----------------------------------------------------------
In backend/llm/executionSessions.py, LlmExecutionBindings.bind validates service/
registry/owners under its lane, releases it to construct LlmExecutionBinding and
ER-01 participants, then reacquires the lane checking only duplicate publication.
It does not revalidate revocation or preserve a pending publication ticket that
close/withdraw can revoke. Host/registry/owner/provider revocation can finish in
that gap; bind then publishes a new binding with revoked=False. Registration
withdrawal before final publication is also not rechecked there.

Consequences: new lifetime ownership can appear after shutdown/unload has already
selected/revoked its bindings. In the host case owners are closed, but the newly
published binding is not marked revoked, so its leaseCloser does not enter normal
binding finalization. This misses the promised structural lifetime invariant even
when later access validation prevents inference. Registration reuse must not make
that late binding an authority source or an unreported lifetime leak.
