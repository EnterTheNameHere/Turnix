P2 / EX-04-R1: final phase alone is insufficient required-capture evidence
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: first-party/applications/experimentRunner/archiveFormat.py:311-332,351-353.
Grammar allows an initial request-evidence 'final' (needed for failed-before-send
diagnostics), then accepts a succeeded turn under captureRequired solely because
turn['phase']=='final'. It forgets whether the exact serialized BODY and attempt
records were validated and forgets final transport/response facts for the success
check. Thus incomplete or contradictory required provenance is accepted as a
complete succeeded archive rather than rejected as an invalid typed sequence.

Independent reproducer starts with an ordinary successful archive from actual
RuntimeHost/Job/provider and synthetic IO, verifies required capture policy, then:
 A. removes every serialized/transport-attempt/headers evidence record, retaining
    final records, successful turns/unit/suite and seal; or
 B. retains every record/body but changes final mayHaveSent to false and final
    response observations to unavailable, contradicting prior observed response
    and succeeded turns.
It rebuilds sequential DATA/COMMIT hashes and the seal's actual suite-outcome
pointer. There are no checksum, missing-reference, sequence or stale-seal failures
to hide the semantic error. Both reads return status='sealed' and
lastObservedSuiteStatus='succeeded'; expected result is explicit corruption at the
first inconsistent record with the preceding valid prefix preserved.

This is not an authenticated-archive or hostile-user tamper-proofing request.
The existing contract explicitly validates typed order/semantics beyond hashes;
its own tests already rehash malformed records to exercise that boundary. Hash
integrity cannot replace those checks. A missing body must not become full required
request provenance, and contradictory local facts must not become a successful
typed protocol. No claim about actual current model execution being forged here.
