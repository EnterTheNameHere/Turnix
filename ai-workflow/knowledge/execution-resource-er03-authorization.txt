1. Lifecycle/attempt authority and shutdown
-------------------------------------------
Authorize the bounded lifecycle.py phase split and new providerComposition.py,
plus the explicitly listed host/runtime/Pack plumbing. Keep existing public
create/load signatures/ordering compatible where no composer is required.
No application hook, creation commit/save, start or public host registration may
precede successful composition. No-composer external/unrelated applications keep
their existing behavior. Managed activation without approved composition fails
before those effects with a clear ownership diagnostic.

The initializing/activating attempt owns the snapshot and rollback. Its ticket
must be revalidated after each composer and before continuation; closing/revoking
an attempt cannot be undone by a late composer return. Public invocation and
activation gates exclude leaked references during prepare/compose, while the
issued continuation permits hooks to call ordinary registered capabilities after
composition. Do not grant LLM execution directly to lifecycle/onLoad Contexts.
Keep capability transaction/IO views and existing Job serialization intact.

Stop must close admission and mark pending attempts before allowing finalization.
Check pending attempts before legacy shared-service/runtime teardown and before
any new resource finalizer that could destroy state still owned by a composer.
Callbacks already in progress retain their exact objects until they unwind; host
or runtime close must not race their abort/unload. Report typed incomplete cleanup
immediately, without waiting for a composer while holding a lane. Do not declare
STOPPED/drained while pending initialization still owns cleanup. Initialization
failure cannot enter resume, persist, or register a runtime after shutdown wins.
Retain sufficient host/standalone roots for unknown cleanup and repeated close.

2. Partial rollback and direct activation
-----------------------------------------
Failure after any subset of composers succeeded must revoke every new binding/
owner/registration and unwind the new activation batch. Attempt all cleanup and
preserve primary exceptions plus bounded pending/unknown disposition. Tickets
transfer only at accepted ER-02/R1 publication; unpublished tickets remain the
composer's responsibility. No silent suppression through _cleanupFailedOperation.
Composition and new provider rollback/stop/wait/finalization run outside enclosing
host/runtime/registry/broker/domain lanes. Legacy arbitrary Pack callbacks are not
claimed globally outside lanes, and must not be broadly refactored in this scope.

Direct activate/activatePack uses the same batch barrier, rejects unsafe reentrant
activation under invocation/lifecycle lanes, and rolls back only its new batch.
Preserve pre-existing owners/bindings/claims and do not close their whole registry
on a later-batch failure. A retry gets fresh identities. Later callers get no
implicit grants and removal/reactivation cannot inherit a former owner's grant.
No new bind/grant/ER-01 admission API or mutation of broker internals is authorized.

3. Stable provider lifetime and operational migration
-----------------------------------------------------
One exact resource service/domain ID must resolve to one stable compatible
adapter/catalog owner even if more than one composer object is configured.
Reject conflicting stable envelopes rather than opening an alternative authority
for the same host domain. Registration-ticket accounting is lifetime only; ER-01
remains the sole claim/reservation/pin/transition authority. New owner grants use
actual loaded identities/Registration objects, never config strings alone.
Retain immutable LC launch/environment/evidence and per-request semantics.

Use the explicit initial transition migration; no legacy managed bypass or hidden
auto-materialization. Update the identified provider/tests/examples and record
consumer-owned adoption requirements. Do not modify consumer branches or claim
their compatibility/adoption verified. Keep old live leases protected from theft,
hot takeover or peer shutdown. Preserve external-provider behavior without
advertising managed protection for an external endpoint.

4. Stream completion, unsupported guarantees and verification
-------------------------------------------------------------
The existing _readEvents emits completed on [DONE]; by itself it does not require
a preceding valid finish_reason and does not retain completion IDs. Do not treat
that completed event plus an idle slot as sufficient new quiescence evidence.
For controlled sessions track the actual native terminal completion for the owned
request, then the consistent completion ID and bounded post-terminal owned-slot
observation proposed in the addendum. Validate frames according to their pinned
protocol shape; heartbeats/usage frames must not invent a second completion.
A bare [DONE], truncated/malformed/foreign-ID stream or pre-terminal idle snapshot
does not release the claim. A valid empty-text completion may still be successful.
Keep cancellation/early-close uncertainty fail-closed with no recovery/restart.
Actual binary/model effects remain separately gated; no synthetic test attests them.

Required retention/reuse/freshness that the stock adapter cannot supply rejects
before provider IO. Best-effort fresh requests report precise attempted effects,
keep compatible weights resident, and never claim hidden-state reset. Preserve
reasoning/result retrieval, native usage, cancellation and opt-in PM/G boundaries.

Implement the proposal/addendum deterministic verification matrix, including both
original residency race Architectural Tests, actual manifest and direct substrate
paths, two Applications, lifecycle hook capability work, no effects before failed
composition, stop/compose races, multi-composer partial failure, later-batch failure
preserving existing work, no-composer regressions and second-thread lane probes.
Prove normal successful completion drains claims and permits explicit transition;
do not satisfy safety solely by quarantining every successful request.

Verify the worker's own Python 3.15 runtime; execute focused, relevant and full
Actant tests, diff/header/docstring checks and report exact commits/commands/counts/
skips/failures/limits. Tests must preserve architectural guarantees while migrating
obsolete managed fixtures. Publish completion to Integration for independent
review/testing/acceptance; do not merge production or self-accept.
