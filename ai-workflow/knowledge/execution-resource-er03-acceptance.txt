-----------------------------------------------

install LlamaCppComposition with host-authored frozen catalogs, exact provider and
caller/helper EntrySelectors, selector grants and separate transition authority.
Pack config cannot mint those rights. Perform ctx.llm.transitionProfile on the
issued profile before first use; there is no implicit first-call load/switch.
Later owners receive no live grant extension. A drained full-plan reactivation
creates new registrations/owners and must compose them anew. See provider README.

through that controlled composition. It does not migrate or verify Completion,
AI Buddy, Materialization Test or Evil Analysis. Their branches/host setup remain
application-owned; notices name this source ref and required adoption work. The
in-repository Evil CLI lacks the new composer and needs its owner's explicit
managed setup migration. Existing external mode keeps its prior behavior, with
no managed guarantees. Do not imply the old managed CLI remains compatible.
