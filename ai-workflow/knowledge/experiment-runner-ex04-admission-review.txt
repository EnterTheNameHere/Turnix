Independent finding / source basis
-----------------------------------
P1: owner revocation in a required pre-send capture callback does not prevent
inference send. Both serialized and transport-attempt callbacks can revoke the
actual caller, ACK and still reach inference open. A later failed Job cannot undo
that request. Existing _Session.requireResident checks neutral claim usability,
which does not synchronously reflect queued owner revocation.

Confirmed backend source: revokeOwner marks owner._closed and queues cleanup;
LlmInvocationAccess.requireValid checks membership/validity/actual owner closure
and cancellation. LlmCallSession.requireValid adds exact binding liveness, mutation
scope, call closure/claim, conversation and retained-context checks. Delegating
this validator avoids a second provider-side implementation of lifecycle rules.
Registration/binding withdrawal similarly belongs to that existing validator.
Do not read private owner._closed fields in provider or alter neutral claim cleanup.

Binding implementation limits
-------------------------------
1. Runtime installs the validator from the exact LlmCallSession that minted this
   admission. No caller-supplied owner ID, ContextVar lookup or other call's cached
   validator is authority. Missing/unbound validation must fail closed when invoked;
   no no-op fallback to claim.usable. Keep callable/identity private, out of repr,
   serialized evidence, metadata, public query/facade and generic telemetry.
2. Delegate existing semantics, including cancellation. Do not mint a new claim,
   grant, reservation or operation and do not reimplement backend checks in provider.
   A retained admission must fail validation after its call/access closes, owner or
   exact binding is revoked, or its existing mutation/conversation lifetime expires.
   Do not retain an invocation Context, transaction or IO facade to implement this.
   Root the callback before adapter use and avoid leaking dead call/scope references.
3. Check after EACH forward-execution required callback and immediately before
   inference open, including disabled/default-off path at that send boundary.
   Keep existing owned residency and exact stop/cancellation checks; validation
   does not replace native/process/generation checks. Later stream use must continue
   to respect existing runtime validity. No lock held across a sink or network send.
4. Normal execution validation and old-ticket cleanup are separate. Do NOT call
   requireValid as a prerequisite for closeExecution, stop, native cleanup, queued
   lifetime draining or final diagnostic callbacks. Revoked admission must still
   finalize using its existing exact cleanup authority. Never clear UNKNOWN from
   this check or require an expired scope to authorize cleanup.
5. This is a boundary recheck, not an atomic revocation-versus-OS-send guarantee.
   Revocation after the final check can still race with send. Preserve that limit;
   do not hold authority locks across transport IO or redesign cancellation/ER-01.
6. Preserve original two regressions unchanged. Add actual backend/provider tests
   for exact registration/binding withdrawal during each pre-send callback, closed
   call/access, normal/default-off behavior and separate cleanup after revocation.
   Cover applicable conversation/mutation lifetime checks using the existing
   validator. Keep cancellation, first-exit, sink failure/privacy and lock tests.
   No monkeypatch as the production implementation or weakening/xfail of failures.
