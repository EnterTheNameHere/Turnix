P2 / R1-A: preserve provider action acknowledgement in report cleanup
--------------------------------------------------------------------
reporting.py:333-337 cleanCleanup uses a vocabulary that excludes 'acknowledged'
and 'attempted', actual ProviderEffect status values. Normal successful actual
managed-call archive has cleanup.status='acknowledged', quiescence='confirmed'.
report() changes only status to 'unknown'. reportSchema.py also excludes the real
status, enforcing the lossy projection; table/JSON/CSV thus cannot preserve it.
Normal retained cleanup evidence is misreported even without archive corruption.

Independent test runs actual App/Job/provider with synthetic IO, reads original
turn cleanup, compares report row to it and verifies CSV acknowledgement retention.
It fails at status unknown versus acknowledged. This is not a missing-data case.
Use the actual action-status domain, with a distinct unavailable marker where no
evidence exists. Preserve status AND quiescence independently through schema/JSON/
CSV/table. Do not infer acknowledgement from quiescence or rename both to a generic
completed/confirmed. Add attempted/failed/unknown/unavailable coverage as applicable;
do not change backend semantics or weaken original UNKNOWN safety.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
observationFormat.py:160-174 validates stream scalar decimal shape but not their
monotonic relationships or the derived firstTextLatencyNs. Consequently archive2
grammar accepts contradictory retained PM facts, and reporting.metrics consumes

Two independent tests begin with a real App/runtime/PM archive using synthetic

Each writes an exact new immutable blob/ref, recomputes all DATA/COMMIT hashes and

this specifically demonstrates missing semantic validation beyond checksums/types.

Validate known same-epoch monotonic bounds, nullable/availability/state consistency
and derived latency from its recorded origin/first delivery. Apply relevant stream
window and observation relationships without equating truncated windows to complete
history, imposing another clock domain, or fabricating absent timestamps. Contradictory
committed snapshots must be rejected before publishing candidate state or producing
available metrics. Preserve preceding valid prefix and selected-view behavior.
Both online writer/capture validation and offline reader use the App codec; no
shared serializer/runtime change is requested. Do not silently repair archived
timestamps or overwrite inconsistent evidence with a derived value and call it valid.
Valid empty/no-first-text/unavailable/partial/cancelled/truncated observations must
remain valid with honest availability, not new inference errors.
