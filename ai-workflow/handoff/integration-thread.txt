Established semantic direction
------------------------------
1. Application-local semantic execution/state remains separate from host-owned shared
   scarce-resource lifetime.

2. RuntimeHost is the natural upper lifetime/composition boundary, but shared domains
   should be separate service objects rather than turning RuntimeHost into a monolith.

3. Do not create a RuntimeHost-specific llama.cpp manager as the final abstraction.
   Shared model/execution residency must be provider-neutral; llama.cpp is the first
   concrete realization.

4. Application provider registration/facades may remain application-local while
   delegating expensive physical runtime/model ownership to a host service.

5. External ingress targets Actant/an Application first. Model acquisition is a
   downstream concern. Logical application addressability must not require model
   residency.

6. Suspension is a resource-state transition, not an authority-state transition.
   Suspension preserves the same ApplicationRun/applicationRunId even if concrete
   Python/runtime objects are destroyed and reconstructed.

7. Active transactions must never commit merely because suspension/resource pressure
   asks work to finish. Unfinished transaction/continuation data may be persisted
   non-authoritatively when safely resumable.

8. Jobs keep ordinary lifecycle semantics across suspension. Completed Jobs remain
   completed. Running Jobs may complete, be preserved, be cancelled, or delay/block
   suspension according to their contract/policy. Cancellation caused by suspension
   must be reported to the owning workflow; Actant must not silently restart arbitrary
   cancelled Jobs.

9. Suspension should quiesce with a configurable grace period rather than immediately
   killing all work. A proving implementation may use a deterministic fallback until
   user-decision/policy infrastructure exists; the grace duration itself is not
   semantic design.
