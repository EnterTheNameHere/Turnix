DECISIONS / BOUNDARIES
----------------------
- This thread owns AI-Buddy-specific application behavior; Integration owns reusable
  Actant/runtime facilities exposed by the application pressure.
- The first user surface may be deliberately primitive: terminal input/history and
  TTS output are sufficient to begin exercising the runtime.
- AI Buddy should not assume one monolithic agent loop. Exploration currently favors
  bounded scheduled execution steps plus potentially concurrent service loops with
  different latency requirements; this remains exploratory rather than final design.
- ASR, TTS, vision, and other specialist operations should be requested by semantic
  capability. Their implementation may be local, Windows-native, dedicated-runtime,
  or cloud; driver location is not AI Buddy semantics.
- ASR itself should return recognized speech/provenance. Speaker recognition, intent,
  interrupt classification, and similar interpretation are separate capabilities or
  AI Buddy processing layers.
- llama.cpp remains the dependable primary local cognition path and Windows is the
  primary platform target. Optional runtimes such as vLLM may add capabilities but
  must not become pillars unless future evidence justifies that change.
- Completion and AI Buddy are expected to coexist; Materialization Test may run
  temporarily beside them. They must not evolve into independently hosted Actant
  universes with mutually unaware model/resource authorities.
- Existing DA-28 and IS-03..IS-06 host/client material is relevant design input. It is
  not frozen implementation instruction: implementation pressure may revise or
  replace those ideas and later reconcile the DA/IS corpus to the better materialized
  design.
