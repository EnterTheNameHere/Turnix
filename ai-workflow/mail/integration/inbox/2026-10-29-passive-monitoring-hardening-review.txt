H-01: FAILED SUBSCRIPTION ACQUISITION
------------------------------------
TelemetryService.subscribe() adds a TelemetrySubscription to _subscriptions before
_collect() and _ensureWorker(). If an unexpected exception escapes either, the
caller never receives the subscription but its collection claim might remain.

Verify with injected exception at initial _collect and worker setup separately.
Acceptance: acquisition is atomic from the caller's perspective, or failed
acquisition reliably revokes its claim, clears retained monitoring data and
does not keep polling/resources active; pre-existing consumers survive.
Normal provider unavailability remains nonfatal/explicit. Test that a later
valid subscription can be acquired after the fault.

H-02: UNEXPECTED POLLING-THREAD TERMINATION
-------------------------------------------
TelemetryService._samplingLoop() sets _thread = None only on normal exit.
An unexpected exception escaping the loop could leave a dead thread reference,
preventing _ensureWorker() from starting replacement polling.

Investigate with deterministic injected faults, distinguishing ordinary
handled sensor failures from unexpected collector/scheduler/clock exceptions.
Acceptance: no permanently stale worker reference and later legitimate
subscription/sampling requests can recover. Preserve one shared collector,
consumer isolation, bounded history, and truthful failure visibility; do not
introduce unbounded automatic retry/spin or mask platform failure evidence.
