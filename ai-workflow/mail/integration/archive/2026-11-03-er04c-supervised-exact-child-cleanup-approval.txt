AUTHORIZED BOUNDARY
1. Prepare and execute the previously scoped operator-supervised local ER-04C
   real-provider cancellation probes, with one isolated Actant-owned model
   process/domain/slot/request per case, exact initial ownership and native
   id_task captured before abort whenever feasible. Test graceful stop FIRST;
   observe the exact bound task/process/slot/generation and distinguish cancel
   request from confirmed task cessation, native idle and Actant claim status.
2. If graceful stop fails, times out, or Actant retains an UNKNOWN claim despite
   native stop, the operator may explicitly perform emergency cleanup of ONLY
   the EXACT Popen child launched and retained for THAT supervised test case.
   Establish immutable owner/process-handle identity and verify it is still the
   same owned child, not an unrelated service or reused PID. Bound terminate,
   wait/reap and (only if terminate failed and explicitly supervised) escalation
   to kill for that same process. Record attempts and actual exit/reap outcome;
   never represent an attempt as confirmed process exit.
3. Retain and report the original CANCELLED/FAILED/UNKNOWN inference/claim outcome.
   Forced process exit is post-test disposal, NOT graceful cancellation evidence,
   not inference success, not restored context and not a reason to erase/repair
   ER-01 unknown tombstones or reuse a quarantined domain. Do not manually
   finish claims or assume clean state from an arbitrary idle observation.
4. Preserve private complete diagnostic evidence and only publish redacted
   structural summaries. If process ownership, target identity, stop disposition
   or operator supervision is uncertain, stop rather than terminate blindly.
   Provide a way to see/report stuck cancellation independently of blocked
   network-read and stop callbacks.
5. Keep all changes narrowly test-harness/operator-control scoped unless actual
   evidence shows a small provider-specific graceful-stop correction is needed.
   Any production ER-03 behavior change or general-purpose recovery API should
   be proposed and reviewed against ER-01/02 ownership guarantees before adoption.

NON-AUTHORIZED
No unattended/automatic process kill or restart; no preemption, arbitrary PID
discovery, port takeover, host.stop/parent-exit workaround, clearing tombstones,
changing provider quiescence guarantees, changing other Applications/consumers,
new scheduler, generic recovery machinery or Experiment Runner implementation.
Do not increase resource ceilings or switch models/binaries silently. Keep the
test-only emergency cleanup strictly separated from regular Actant runtime
behavior and report unsupported/inconclusive cancellation honestly.

assignments/execution-resource-cancellation-probe.txt. Compare header-free
