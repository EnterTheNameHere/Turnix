Materialized behavior:
- callers may retain a stable Job handle before blocking ApplicationRuntime.runJob()
  execution and request cancellation from another thread;
- cancellation request is distinct from terminal Job outcome;
- Pending Jobs may become Cancelled immediately;
- Running Jobs remain Running until execution acknowledges cancellation;
- the Job-owned CancellationSignal propagates through CodeEntryContext;
- Pack code may cooperatively observe/check ctx.cancellation;
- LLM execution carries the same signal through LlmProcessingPipeline and
  LlmCallRequest;
- cancelled ProcessingRuns are classified as Cancelled and abort unresolved
  speculative processing state;
- first-party llama.cpp realizes active inference stop by closing the current HTTP
  response while generic runtime layers own lifecycle classification.

Current limits:
- model startup/readiness inside getExecutionProfile()/ensureModel() is not yet
  interruptible;
- arbitrary long-running Pack work must cooperatively observe ctx.cancellation;
- cancellation does not yet imply model priority, eviction, or arbitration.
