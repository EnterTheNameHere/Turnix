The cancellation path is Job-owned and provider-neutral:
- a caller may retain a Job handle before runJob() blocks;
- requestCancellation() records a request separately from terminal outcome;
- Running work remains Running until execution acknowledges cancellation;
- CodeEntryContext exposes ctx.cancellation for cooperative Pack work;
- LLM calls carry the same signal into provider execution;
- cancelled ProcessingRuns roll back unresolved speculative state and are not
  classified as Failed;
- llama.cpp closes only its active inference HTTP response as the concrete stop action.

This gives AI Buddy a reusable interruption mechanism without embedding AI-Buddy policy
inside the provider or host.

Current limits:
- managed model startup/readiness is still not interruptible;
- priority/latency classes and model eviction/arbitration are not implemented;
- cancellation does not silently restart work.
