==============================================================================
01. VERDICT
==============================================================================

Recommendation verdict:
    Superseded by Proposal P02 for SavePack as a current Pack kind and persistence
    identity; useful only as translated boundary-preservation guidance.

    The recommendation correctly preserves DA-10's core identity and visibility
    model: Pack identity layers stay distinct, declaredId is local, packTreeId is
    hierarchical and not filesystem-based, author is identity, version is identity
    input rather than compatibility magic, hierarchy is containment rather than
    authority, visibility is eligibility to be seen, exportNestedPacks controls
    direct-child export only, importPacksFromParent is import-visibility rather
    than authority, visibility is not resolution, visibility does not grant
    capability authority, and PackManager presentation is not visibility authority.

    However, the recommendation still treats SavePack as a current Pack kind and
    recommends keeping SavePack visibility as-is. That model is superseded by the
    SaveBundle model. Translate SavePack-local visibility into SaveBundle-local
    retained/copied Pack material visibility and remove SavePack as a current Pack
    kind unless a compatibility alias is explicitly retained elsewhere.

Document verdict:
    Keep with targeted but important SaveBundle rewrite.

DA-10 is architecturally strong as the Pack identity, hierarchy, and computed
visibility document.

No heavy conceptual rewrite is recommended.

The main required fixes are:

    - remove SavePack from the current Pack kind list unless a compatibility alias
      is explicitly retained
    - state that SaveBundle is not a Pack and is not ordinary globally visible
      Pack material
    - translate SavePack visibility into SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack
      material visibility
    - translate SavePack-context wording into SaveBundle/Application context
      wording
    - keep SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material isolated from global
      visibility by default
    - keep ordinary content-root precedence from overriding SaveBundle-local
      retained/copied Pack material by default
    - keep DA-10 focused on identity, hierarchy, visibility declarations, and
      computed visibility
    - keep DA-15 as owner of visibility enforcement and access-control
      consequences
    - keep DA-13 as owner of dependency-source policy
    - avoid final-document version/process metadata leakage

Primary conclusion:
    Keep DA-10, but rewrite it out of the SavePack-as-Pack-kind layer. Preserve
    the identity/hierarchy/visibility model and translate save-local visibility
    into SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material visibility.

==============================================================================
01A. OBSOLETE-PRESERVATION CHECK
==============================================================================

DA-10 should not keep older terminology merely because it appears in the older
source document or recommendation.

Specifically, the rewrite should not preserve:

    - SavePack as a current Pack kind
    - SavePack as durable persistence identity
    - SavePack visibility as if SavePack were an ordinary Pack visibility category
    - SavePack-local copied Packs as globally visible ordinary Pack material
    - SavePack-local copied Packs as subject to ordinary global custom precedence
      by default
    - SavePack/AppInstance selected-graph wording where SaveBundle/Application or
      ApplicationRun context is meant
    - AppInstance as runtime identity vocabulary
    - PackManager UI display as visibility authority
    - visibility as runtime execution authority
    - visibility as dependency success
    - visibility as capability permission or service access
    - hierarchy containment as authority inheritance

If a legacy alias is intentionally retained, DA-10 should scope it explicitly and
should still state the canonical architecture term as primary.

Otherwise, the old terms and assumptions should be removed or translated.

The useful part of the old recommendation is not the old SavePack terminology.
The useful part is the visibility boundary:

    Save-local retained/copied Pack material must not leak into ordinary global
    Pack visibility merely because it exists inside a durable persistence closure.
    It remains local to the relevant SaveBundle/Application context unless an
    owning document explicitly exposes it.

==============================================================================
01B. DOCUMENT-METADATA LEAKAGE CHECK
==============================================================================

The final DA-10 document should read as architecture documentation, not as audit,
version, or rewrite-process metadata.

Do not include statements such as:

    - This is V3 design.
    - In V3, SaveBundle replaces SavePack.
    - The new design removes SavePack from Pack kinds.
    - This rewrite changed SavePack visibility.
    - Unlike V2, SaveBundle is not a Pack.
    - Codex kept SavePack wording.
    - This audit decided to remove SavePack.
    - DA-10 was rewritten because of the audit.

Those facts may be useful in audit files, branch notes, migration notes,
compatibility notes, changelogs, or editor instructions. They do not belong in
the architecture document itself.

The final DA-10 should simply define Pack identity layers, hierarchy, author and
version identity, visibility declarations, computed global visibility,
parent-import visibility, capability/visibility separation, DA-15 enforcement
separation, and SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material visibility.

==============================================================================
02. WHAT DA-10 GETS RIGHT
==============================================================================

02.1. Pack identity layers are distinct
---------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly separates declaredId, packTreeId, author, and version.

It correctly says declaredId is local, not globally unique.

It correctly says packTreeId is hierarchical, computed from hierarchy, and not
filesystem-based.

Preserve this.

02.2. Author is identity
------------------------

DA-10 correctly says author is part of identity rather than decoration.

It correctly says author is computed from declaration or inheritance and that
unresolved author identity makes the Pack invalid.

Preserve this.

02.3. Version is identity input
-------------------------------

DA-10 correctly separates declaredVersion from parsed version identity and says
invalid version syntax causes Pack rejection.

It correctly says version affects Pack identity and later resolution
compatibility, but DA-10 does not own resolution winner selection.

Preserve this.

02.4. Hierarchy is containment, not authority
---------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly says hierarchy contributes to packTreeId construction and does
not by itself grant visibility, capability authority, activation, permission, or
runtime execution order.

Preserve this strongly.

02.5. Visibility is eligibility to be seen
------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly says visibility answers whether a Pack is eligible to be seen
from another Pack context.

It correctly says visibility is not dependency declaration, import use,
activation order, capability authority, dependency satisfaction, permission
grant, runtime execution order, or resolution winner selection.

Preserve this.

02.6. exportNestedPacks is direct-child export only
---------------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly defines exportNestedPacks as direct-child export control.

It correctly allows false, true, or a list of direct-child declaredId values.

It correctly rejects dotted hierarchical IDs inside exportNestedPacks and avoids
ambiguous deep export semantics.

Preserve this.

02.7. Computed global visibility is not resolution
--------------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly defines computed global visibility after hierarchy is known and
says it is static relative to the discovered/indexed Pack tree.

It correctly says computed visibility is not runtime-loaded state and not a
runtime resolution outcome.

Preserve this.

02.8. importPacksFromParent is import-visibility, not authority
---------------------------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly says importPacksFromParent controls which parent-context Packs
are import-visible to a child Pack.

It correctly says importPacksFromParent does not imply export, capability
inheritance, or permission inheritance.

Preserve this and add the DA-12/DA-16 stop-line that it also does not grant
native import authority, service access, capability invocation, or activation.

02.9. Visibility / resolution split is strong
---------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly says a Pack may exist but be invisible, a visible Pack may still
fail resolution, and visibility is not dependency satisfaction.

Preserve this.

02.10. Capability / visibility split is strong
----------------------------------------------

DA-10 correctly says capability exports are not inherited through visibility
alone, capability imports remain explicit, and parent visibility does not grant
capability authority.

Preserve this and keep DA-16 as owner of capability, service, and permission
semantics.

==============================================================================
03. MAJOR REQUIRED FIX: SAVELOCAL VISIBILITY WITHOUT SAVEPACK AS PACK KIND
==============================================================================

The target DA-10 currently says:

    AppPack, ModPack, ViewPack, ContentPack, and SavePack are specific pack kinds.

    Current default local visibility includes SavePack -> private.

    SavePack visibility remains save-local.

    Packs copied into a SavePack do not leak into global visibility merely
    because they exist inside that SavePack.

    Save-local copied packs may override external candidates within that SavePack
    context.

Proposal P02 changes the durable persistence object to SaveBundle. SaveBundle is
not a Pack.

DA-10 must preserve the isolation semantics but not the SavePack-as-Pack-kind
model.

03.1. Remove SavePack from current Pack kind list
-------------------------------------------------

Recommended current Pack-kind statement:

    Pack is the general container concept in Turnix.

    AppPack, ModPack, ViewPack, and ContentPack are Pack kinds.

If another current document intentionally keeps a legacy SavePack alias, DA-10
may mention it only as a compatibility alias and should still say the primary
current persistence identity is SaveBundle.

Do not list SaveBundle as a Pack kind.

03.2. Replace SavePack default visibility
-----------------------------------------

Remove:

    SavePack -> private

from ordinary Pack-kind default local visibility unless a compatibility alias is
explicitly retained.

Recommended replacement:

    SaveBundle is not assigned ordinary Pack-kind local visibility because
    SaveBundle is not a Pack.

    SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material follows the retained Pack's
    identity and visibility rules within the SaveBundle/Application context, plus
    any SaveBundle-local source/graph rules owned by DA-13 and persistence
    documents.

03.3. Replace `SAVEPACK VISIBILITY` section
-------------------------------------------

Suggested section title:

    SAVEBUNDLE-LOCAL PACK MATERIAL VISIBILITY

or:

    SAVEBUNDLE-LOCAL VISIBILITY CONSTRAINTS

Recommended content:

    SaveBundle is not a Pack and is not ordinary globally visible Pack material.

    SaveBundle-local retained or copied Pack material remains local to the
    relevant SaveBundle/Application context unless an owning document explicitly
    exposes it.

    SaveBundle-local retained or copied Pack material does not leak into global
    visibility merely because it exists inside a SaveBundle.

    SaveBundle-local retained or copied Pack material may override or shadow
    external/global candidates only within the relevant SaveBundle/Application
    context according to DA-13 dependency-source and selected-graph rules.

    Ordinary global content-root precedence does not override SaveBundle-local
    retained or copied Pack material by default.

    Tooling and PackManager UI presentation of SaveBundle-local retained/copied
    Pack material does not grant global visibility or runtime authority.

03.4. Keep exact internal SaveBundle visibility open if not owned here
----------------------------------------------------------------------

DA-10 may keep open questions, but they should use current names:

    - exact internal visibility rules for SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack
      material
    - interaction with exportNestedPacks inside a SaveBundle/Application context
    - tooling and UI representation of SaveBundle-local visibility graphs

The open question should not say SavePack unless explicitly scoped as legacy.

==============================================================================
04. DA-10 / DA-15 SPLIT
==============================================================================

DA-10 and DA-15 overlap around visibility. Preserve the split:

    DA-10 owns:
        Pack identity, hierarchy, visibility declarations, computed visibility,
        parent-import visibility, capability/visibility separation, and the
        structural meaning of SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material
        visibility.

    DA-15 owns:
        visibility enforcement, access-control consequences, reference permission
        failure, source-domain isolation at use points, and visibility as applied
        during dependency resolution, resolvePack, and turnixImport.

DA-10 may define what visibility means and how it is computed.

DA-10 should not become the document that owns enforcement mechanics,
PermissionDeniedError taxonomy, runtime reference checks, dependency-resolution
failure behavior, resolvePack behavior, or turnixImport failure behavior.

==============================================================================
05. REQUIRED OWNER STOP-LINES
==============================================================================

DA-10 should stay the Pack identity, hierarchy, and visibility declaration
mechanics document.

It may name other domains to protect stop-lines, but it should not define their
detailed behavior.

Recommended owner mapping:

    DA-09 owns root discovery boundaries and discovered candidate production.

    DA-11 owns resolution winner selection, source selection, selector resolution,
    and deterministic resolution semantics.

    DA-12 owns importing, module loading, and native/runtime import authority.

    DA-13 owns dependency semantics, PackRequest dependency interpretation,
    dependency-source policy, SaveOnly, GlobalPinned, GlobalNormal, and advisory
    relation semantics.

    DA-14 owns activation lifecycle, activation ordering, activation barriers,
    and activation failure semantics.

    DA-15 owns visibility enforcement and access-control consequences.

    DA-16 owns capability schema, permission prompts, enforcement mechanics,
    service access rules, and capability authority.

    DA-17 owns user decision and remembered policy handling.

    DA-20 owns Application/ApplicationRun lifecycle and runtime identity.

    DA-21 owns RPC, channel, and transport semantics.

    DA-23 owns ExecutionAdapter boundary semantics.

    DA-24 owns OrchestrationUnit and mutation-authority semantics.

    DA-27 owns adapter lifecycle, trust, isolation, failure, and disconnect
    semantics.

    DA-28 owns RuntimeHost workspace ownership and command-surface semantics.

    DA-29 owns PackManager inspection, UI presentation, graph preparation,
    repair, and PackManager-domain trace semantics.

    SaveBundle and persistence documents own SaveBundle storage, retained Pack
    material, copied Pack material, graph evidence, persistence compatibility,
    and durable recovery details.

DA-10 may name these concepts when needed to protect Pack identity, hierarchy,
and visibility boundaries, but it should not define their detailed behavior.

09. ACCEPTANCE CONDITIONS FOR THE REVISED DA-10
==============================================================================

A revised DA-10 is acceptable when all of the following are true:

    - It preserves Pack as the general container concept.

    - It preserves declaredId, packTreeId, author, and version as distinct identity
      layers.

    - It preserves declaredId as local, not globally unique.

    - It preserves packTreeId as hierarchical and not filesystem-based.

    - It preserves author as identity, not decoration.

    - It preserves explicit author resolution failure.

    - It preserves version identity as resolution input, not compatibility magic.

    - It preserves hierarchy as containment, not authority.

    - It preserves visibility as eligibility to be seen.

    - It preserves computed global visibility as static relative to the discovered
      / indexed Pack tree.

    - It preserves exportNestedPacks as direct-child export control only.

    - It preserves importPacksFromParent as import-visibility, not authority.

    - It preserves visibility / resolution separation.

    - It preserves capability / visibility separation.

    - It preserves service / visibility separation.

    - It removes SavePack as a current Pack kind unless an explicitly scoped
      compatibility alias is retained.

    - It does not classify SaveBundle as a Pack kind.

    - It removes SavePack from ordinary Pack-kind default visibility unless a
      compatibility alias is explicitly scoped.

    - It translates SavePack-local visibility into SaveBundle-local retained /
      copied Pack material visibility.

    - It prevents SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material from leaking into
      global visibility by default.

    - It prevents ordinary global custom precedence from overriding
      SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material by default.

    - It keeps PackManager UI presentation from granting global visibility or
      runtime authority.

    - It keeps DA-09 as owner of discovery.

    - It keeps DA-11 as owner of generic resolution and winner/source selection.

    - It keeps DA-12 as owner of importing/module-loading authority.

    - It keeps DA-13 as owner of dependency-source policy.

    - It keeps DA-15 as owner of visibility enforcement and access-control
      consequences.

    - It keeps DA-16 as owner of capability, service, and permission semantics.

    - It keeps DA-20 as owner of Application/ApplicationRun lifecycle.

    - It keeps DA-29 as owner of PackManager inspection, UI presentation, graph
      preparation, repair, and PackManager-domain trace semantics.

    - It keeps SaveBundle storage, retained material, copied material, graph
      evidence, persistence compatibility, and recovery details owned by
      SaveBundle/persistence documents.

    - It does not define concrete PackRequest grammar, resolution algorithm,

10. FINAL EDITOR INSTRUCTION
==============================================================================

Keep DA-10.

Do not perform a heavy conceptual rewrite.

Do perform a targeted rewrite of SavePack/AppInstance terminology and save-local
visibility semantics.

Use REWRITE-DA-10 only as translated boundary-preservation guidance. Do not keep
it as an obsolete historical artifact and do not copy its stale SavePack or
AppInstance vocabulary into the rewritten DA.

DA-10 documentation. The final DA-10 should not say this is V3 design, in V3,
new design, old design, rewritten design, created by Codex, decided by audit, or
part of a rewrite-history process.

DA-10 should remain the Pack identity, hierarchy, visibility declaration,
computed visibility, parent-import visibility, capability/visibility separation,
and SaveBundle-local retained/copied Pack material visibility document.

DA-10 should not become:

    - root discovery specification
    - generic resolution/winner/source-selection specification
    - dependency-source policy specification
    - importing/module-loading authority specification
    - activation lifecycle specification
    - visibility-enforcement/access-control consequence specification
    - capability/service/permission specification
    - Application/ApplicationRun lifecycle specification
    - RPC/channel/transport specification
    - ExecutionAdapter or adapter lifecycle specification
    - RuntimeHost specification
    - OrchestrationUnit/mutation-authority specification
    - PackManager operation/UI/repair specification
    - SaveBundle storage/recovery specification
    - concrete visibility graph visualization or error taxonomy specification

The main work is to preserve DA-10's strong identity/hierarchy/visibility model
while migrating fully out of the SavePack-as-Pack-kind layer into SaveBundle-local
retained/copied Pack material visibility semantics.
