PRESERVATION RULE 1: WINDOW HISTORY IS NOT HIDDEN CORRECTNESS DEPENDENCY
========================================================================

Preserve the central question:

    How does Turnix preserve bounded committed-state history and bounded
    historical evidence without making retained history a hidden correctness
    dependency?

Windows distinguish:

    - authoritative committed boundaries
    - non-authoritative in-flight activity
    - non-authoritative historical evidence

Finalized Windows preserve:

    - what was committed
    - what was aborted
    - what evidence remains retained for explanation, audit, and offline
      inspection

Historical evidence may support explanation, audit, and debugging.

Historical evidence does not become a hidden correctness dependency.

This document defines how finalized Windows relate to authoritative state and
retained evidence.

It does not redefine authoritative state.


PRESERVATION RULE 2: JOURNAL IS NON-AUTHORITATIVE EVIDENCE
==========================================================

Preserve journal status.

Journal content is:

    - append-oriented
    - non-authoritative
    - non-blocking
    - evidence-oriented rather than correctness-defining

Journal data is not state.

Journal data is not required for correctness.

Journal loss does not change committed-state meaning.

Journal retention follows explicit best-effort policy.

Journal writes may be dropped rather than block execution when storage is
unavailable or under severe pressure.

Journal pruning or dropped writes remain trace-visible and classified.

Journal retention class affects garbage collection only.

Journal retention policy does not alter authority boundaries.


PRESERVATION RULE 3: WINDOW SEQUENCE IS EXPLICIT
================================================

Preserve the ordered Window sequence.

An AppInstance maintains one explicit ordered Window sequence.

Every Window in that sequence is exactly one of:

    - SafePointWindow
    - AbortedWindow
    - ActiveWindow

At most one ActiveWindow exists for one AppInstance at a time.

If an ActiveWindow exists, it is the unique mutable tail of the Window sequence.

SafePointWindow and AbortedWindow are finalized Window forms.

ActiveWindow is not finalized.

Window ordering is explicit.

Window ordering does not rely on:

    - timestamps
    - inference
    - presentation order

Window ordering is distinct from Job lifecycle ordering.

Window ordering is distinct from OrchestrationUnit lifecycle ordering.

Job and OrchestrationUnit lifecycle evidence remains correlated to Windows
through trace and retained evidence rather than timestamp inference.


PRESERVATION RULE 4: SAFEPOINTWINDOW IS COMMITTED BOUNDARY
==========================================================

Preserve SafePointWindow semantics.

A SafePointWindow is a finalized authoritative committed boundary.

A SafePointWindow contains committed layers and committed config as
authoritative material.

The authoritative portion of a SafePointWindow contains committed state material
only.

Transactional layer contents are not part of the authoritative SafePointWindow
boundary.

A retained SafePointWindow includes retained trace identity and scope evidence
sufficient to identify:

    - the SafePointWindow itself
    - the committed scope or scopes it covers
    - its relation to the committed work it finalizes

Optional retained evidence may include:

    - revision history
    - journal material
    - additional trace evidence
    - Job lifecycle evidence compatible with retained scope
    - OrchestrationUnit lifecycle evidence compatible with retained scope
    - other non-authoritative evidence compatible with DA-08

Optional retained evidence does not add authority.

Trace, journal, revision, Job lifecycle, and OrchestrationUnit lifecycle
material retained within a SafePointWindow may be truncated or removed according
to explicit policy.

Retention reduction within a SafePointWindow does not invalidate the retained
authoritative committed-state boundary, provided enough authoritative material
remains to restore that SafePointWindow deterministically.

A retained SafePointWindow is not treated as complete if retained trace identity
and scope evidence are insufficient to identify the Window and its committed
scope.

A Job reaching Succeeded does not by itself create a SafePointWindow.

An OrchestrationUnit reaching Completed does not by itself create a
SafePointWindow.

A transaction commit does not by itself create a SafePointWindow.


PRESERVATION RULE 5: ACTIVEWINDOW IS NON-AUTHORITATIVE IN-FLIGHT REGION
=======================================================================

Preserve ActiveWindow semantics.

ActiveWindow is the current in-flight region after the last finalized committed
boundary.

ActiveWindow contains activity that has not yet been finalized as a
SafePointWindow or AbortedWindow.

ActiveWindow may contain:

    - trace events emitted after the last finalized SafePointWindow
    - journal entries produced after the last finalized SafePointWindow
    - metadata about active OrchestrationUnits sufficient for diagnosis
    - metadata about active Jobs sufficient for diagnosis when Jobs exist
    - transactional staging layers and in-flight transactional state

ActiveWindow is non-authoritative.

ActiveWindow does not define committed truth.

ActiveWindow may be discarded during recovery.

ActiveWindow is not interpreted as a deterministic replay boundary.

ActiveWindow does not guarantee deterministic reconstruction of:

    - in-flight activity
    - active or pending Job execution
    - active OrchestrationUnit execution
    - external side effects
    - pre-crash execution ordering beyond what later committed boundaries
      explicitly retain

ActiveWindow evidence does not convert Pending or Running Jobs into replayable
work.


PRESERVATION RULE 6: ABORTEDWINDOW IS NON-COMMIT FINALIZED EVIDENCE
===================================================================

Preserve AbortedWindow semantics.

An AbortedWindow is a finalized ActiveWindow that did not produce a new
authoritative committed boundary.

An AbortedWindow may retain:

    - trace evidence describing what happened after the previous finalized
      SafePointWindow
    - finalization outcome identifying the abort path and reason category
    - journal evidence
    - Job lifecycle evidence compatible with retained scope
    - OrchestrationUnit lifecycle evidence compatible with retained scope
    - other non-authoritative evidence compatible with DA-08

An AbortedWindow does not contain authoritative committed-state changes as part
of its finalized outcome.

An AbortedWindow does not preserve transactional layer contents as authoritative
material.

An AbortedWindow is finalized evidence of non-commit outcome, not a committed
boundary.

An AbortedWindow does not acquire authority merely because it is finalized,
retained, or exported.

If an AbortedWindow is retained at all, it retains enough trace meaning to
explain:

    - that the Window was aborted rather than committed
    - why it was aborted
    - how it relates causally to the retained evidence still present

A Job reaching Failed, Cancelled, TimedOut, or Superseded does not by itself
create an AbortedWindow.

An OrchestrationUnit reaching Failed, Cancelled, or Superseded does not by
itself create an AbortedWindow.

A transaction abort does not by itself create an AbortedWindow.


PRESERVATION RULE 7: FINALIZATION PRODUCES EXACTLY ONE OUTCOME
==============================================================

Preserve finalization rules.

ActiveWindow finalizes as exactly one outcome:

    - SafePointWindow
    - AbortedWindow

Finalization never produces both outcomes for the same ActiveWindow.

Finalization never produces a partial finalized artifact.

Finalization never preserves an unresolved mixed state in which a Window is
partly authoritative and partly aborted.

Finalization into SafePointWindow requires that all transactions relevant to
the finalized committed boundary are resolved.

Finalization into AbortedWindow occurs when in-flight work concludes without
producing a new SafePointWindow and the authoritative committed state remains
that of the last finalized SafePointWindow.

Finalization preserves the distinction between:

    - committed authoritative outcome
    - non-authoritative retained evidence about failed or aborted work

Job terminal outcome does not by itself define Window finalization.

OrchestrationUnit terminal outcome does not by itself define Window
finalization.

Transaction commit or abort does not by itself define Window finalization.

Including evidence about Job, OrchestrationUnit, or transaction outcomes does
not make that evidence authoritative committed state.


PRESERVATION RULE 8: SAFEPOINTWINDOW CREATION MODES ARE EXPLICIT
================================================================

Preserve SafePointWindow request mode separation.

Turnix may support multiple SafePointWindow creation request modes.

Request-mode semantics are explicit.

Request-mode semantics are trace-visible.

Turnix does not silently substitute one request mode for another.

ImmediateCommitted:

    - attempts SafePointWindow creation without waiting for quiescence
    - succeeds immediately if eligible
    - fails deterministically with explicit reason if not eligible
    - does not silently degrade into waiting behavior

AtQuiescence:

    - attempts SafePointWindow creation only when no OrchestrationUnit is active
      for the target AppInstance
    - may queue the request until quiescence is reached
    - queued requests are explicit
    - queued requests are trace-visible
    - queued AtQuiescence does not mean queued Job execution
    - queued AtQuiescence does not alter Job lifecycle state by itself

AtQuiescence waiting is bounded by explicit policy.

Turnix does not silently wait forever.

Turnix does not silently cancel in-flight work to satisfy SafePointWindow
creation.

Turnix does not silently downgrade AtQuiescence into ImmediateCommitted.

SafePointWindow creation timeout is not Job timeout.

SafePointWindow creation cancellation is not Job cancellation.

If Window creation policy affects a Job or OrchestrationUnit, that effect
remains governed by the Job, OrchestrationUnit, and policy documents that own
those lifecycle outcomes.


PRESERVATION RULE 9: WINDOW TRACE CORRELATION IS EXPLICIT CAUSAL IDENTITY
=========================================================================

Preserve trace correlation rules.

DA-08 consumes the trace substrate defined by DA-03.

DA-08 defines Window-specific trace obligations.

DA-08 does not redefine:

    - trace event structure
    - trace DAG structure
    - span semantics
    - trace storage semantics

Finalized Windows are correlated to trace evidence through explicit retained
trace identity and causal relationships rather than through timestamps alone.

Each SafePointWindow is identifiable through retained trace evidence that
states:

    - safePointWindowId
    - committed scope or scopes covered
    - reason or creation path
    - whether optional evidence classes are included by policy

Each AbortedWindow is identifiable through retained trace evidence that states:

    - abortedWindowId
    - abort outcome
    - abort reason category
    - causal relation to retained evidence still present

Trace incompleteness, pruning, corruption, or partial export remains explicit
when it affects retained finalized evidence.

Job lifecycle evidence retained inside finalized Window evidence remains
non-authoritative evidence unless it is also part of committed state under
DA-04.

OrchestrationUnit evidence retained inside finalized Window evidence remains
non-authoritative evidence unless it is also part of committed state under
DA-04.


PRESERVATION RULE 10: EXPORTS AND OFFLINE WORKFLOWS ARE DA-25 / DA-26
=====================================================================

Preserve DA-25 / DA-26 ownership.

DA-25 defines:

    - WindowManifest
    - WindowExport
    - Minimal Audit Bundle
    - optional digest representation
    - partial export
    - missing material
    - offline inspection artifact semantics

DA-26 defines:

    - equality modes
    - ComparisonResult
    - Replay
    - Resume
    - Retry
    - cross-session audit workflow semantics

A Window finalization outcome does not by itself create:

    - WindowExport
    - Minimal Audit Bundle
    - comparison work
    - Replay
    - Resume
    - Retry
    - audit workflow work

Artifact and workflow documents consume Window finalization semantics.

They do not redefine Window finalization.

DA-08 remains authority for:

    - Window kind
    - Window ordering
    - Window finalization
    - Window-domain trace reason ownership
