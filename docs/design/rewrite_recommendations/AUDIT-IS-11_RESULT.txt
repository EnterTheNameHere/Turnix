IS document verdict:
    Keep with targeted terminology, graph-intent, activation-boundary, and
    persistence-boundary fixes.

    IS-11 is a useful concrete implementation specification for ResolvedPackGraph
    and ActivationPlan records. It should not be rewritten from scratch.

    Its strongest invariant is correct: ResolvedPackGraph is selected graph intent
    and ActivationPlan is ordering/readiness intent. Neither record performs
    discovery, generic resolution, importing, activation, capability permission,
    execution authorization, or persistence authority.

    Required fixes are targeted: replace obsolete SavePack, AppInstance, and
    PackContext terminology; align graph context and save graph evidence with
    SaveBundle / Application / ApplicationRun terminology; preserve the boundary
    between evidence/intent records and owning runtime behavior; and ensure
    activation-plan readiness/order groups cannot be read as actual activation,
    concurrency permission, adapter authorization, or lifecycle success.

==============================================================================
WHAT THE DOCUMENT GETS RIGHT
==============================================================================

1. Correct implementation-specification scope.

    IS-11 correctly defines record shapes for selected graph and activation-plan
    handoff without defining root discovery, pack identity/visibility, generic
    PackRequest resolution, import/module loading, dependency/provider/advisory
    semantics, activation lifecycle, capability execution, policy, Job lifecycle,
    Application lifecycle, SaveBundle binding, RPC, UI/View runtime, adapter
    authority, RuntimeHost lifecycle, PackManager boundaries, concrete catalog
    envelopes, Default Launcher behavior, activation executor implementation,
    package management protocol, or JSON Schema publication format.

2. Strong record-type boundary.

    ResolvedPackGraph records selected graph intent.

    ActivationPlan records activation ordering and readiness derived from one
    ResolvedPackGraph.

    One ResolvedPackGraph may have zero, one, or multiple ActivationPlans over
    time. Each ActivationPlan references exactly one ResolvedPackGraph.

3. Strong non-execution boundary.

    ResolvedPackGraph is not discovery, generic resolution, importing, or
    activation.

    ActivationPlan is not activation, does not call onLoad(ctx), and does not
    create authority by itself.

    Selected pack entries are not manifests, not executable code, and do not
    activate packs.

4. Good evidence/intent boundary.

    The records are evidence and intent records. They do not grant permission or
    authority by themselves.

    Mechanical loaders, importers, adapter artifact loaders, and activation
    executors consume or reference these records only through their own authority
    boundaries.

5. Good graph-status and plan-status boundaries.

    graphStatus is not activation outcome, not Job terminal outcome, and not
    lifecycle outcome.

    activationPlanStatus is not activation outcome and not Job terminal outcome.

6. Good dependency/provider/advisory boundaries.

    Dependency edges record evidence and do not perform resolution by themselves.

    Provider-role semantics remain owned by DA-13.

    Advisory evidence does not create hard dependency semantics or activation
    failure by itself.

7. Good accepted-override boundaries.

    Accepted overrides do not make risky graphs safe, do not grant capability
    permission unless DA-16 and DA-17 explicitly define that effect, and do not
    grant adapter execution authorization.

8. Good source-evidence boundary.

    Source evidence does not prove authenticity by itself and does not grant
    authority.

9. Good readiness and blocker boundaries.

    Ready does not guarantee activation success.

    Blockers do not perform decision handling by themselves and do not become
    activation failure unless activation is attempted or the owning workflow
    classifies the plan as failed.

10. Good ordering/concurrency boundary.

    Activation order preparation does not activate packs. Groups do not imply
    concurrent activation unless a future authoritative concurrency mode defines
    that behavior.

11. Good stored-graph evidence boundary.

    Stored graph records do not guarantee that the same pack material is
    currently available and do not bypass fresh dependency resolution where DA-13
    requires fresh resolution.

12. Good trace boundary.

    ResolvedPackGraph and ActivationPlan creation remain trace-visible. Graph and
    activation-plan trace references do not replace the underlying trace records.

13. Good open-question discipline.

    Exact identifier formats, graph hashing, JSON Schema publication, storage
    layouts, activation executor envelopes, validation/readiness vocabularies,
    ordering evidence, warning/diagnostic vocabularies, and export/bundling
    representation remain open.

==============================================================================
MAJOR SEMANTIC RISKS
==============================================================================

1. SavePack terminology is superseded by SaveBundle.

    IS-11 uses SavePack throughout:

        - SavePack graph evidence
        - SavePack visibility semantics
        - SavePack binding behavior
        - SavePack graph storage specifications
        - graphContext savePackId
        - SavePack graph references
        - SaveCopiedPack / SavePinnedPack
        - SaveOnly source model
        - NewSaveCreation / ExistingSaveLoad / SaveCompatibilityCheck /
          SaveRepair / SaveExport
        - SavePackGraphMismatch
        - stored SavePack graph evidence

    Current source-stack correction:
        Proposal P02 defines SaveBundle as the durable persistence closure of one
        Application. SaveBundle is not a Pack, not Application, not
        ApplicationRun, not RuntimeHost, not live process snapshot, not arbitrary
        writable directory, not Job store, and not authority by itself.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Translate SavePack to SaveBundle where the meaning is durable persistence
        closure, save identity, stored graph evidence, copied/pinned retained pack
        material, compatibility evidence, repair evidence, or export/bundling
        context.

        Likely replacements:

            savePackId
                -> saveBundleId

            savePackGraphReferences
                -> saveBundleGraphReferences

            SavePackGraphMismatch
                -> SaveBundleGraphMismatch

            SavePack graph storage layout
                -> SaveBundle graph storage layout

            SaveCopiedPack / SavePinnedPack
                -> SaveBundleCopiedPack / SaveBundlePinnedPack, or copied/pinned
                   pack references retained by a SaveBundle

            SaveOnly
                -> SaveBundleOnly or save-bound source model, depending on final
                   DA-13 terminology

        Do not preserve SavePack as current terminology unless explicitly scoped
        as compatibility alias.

2. AppInstance terminology is superseded by Application / ApplicationRun.

    IS-11 uses AppInstance in graph context, status boundaries, activation
    context, trace links, and unsupported/locked summaries.

    Current source-stack correction:
        Proposal P02 defines Application as the durable application individual and
        ApplicationRun as one live execution period of an Application.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Replace AppInstance with Application and/or ApplicationRun according to
        meaning.

        Likely replacements:

            appInstanceId
                -> applicationId and/or applicationRunId

            AppInstance lifecycle outcome
                -> Application lifecycle outcome and/or ApplicationRun lifecycle
                   outcome; usually ApplicationRun where live runtime state is
                   meant

            AppInstance operation evidence
                -> Application/ApplicationRun lifecycle or operation evidence

            activationContext AppInstance
                -> Application/ApplicationRun context

3. PackContext terminology is superseded by ModCallContext / invocation-scoped
   Pack gateway terminology.

    IS-11 says ActivationPlan does not create PackContext authority and activation
    entries do not grant PackContext authority.

    Current source-stack correction:
        P02 replaces PackContext with ModCallContext as invocation-scoped
        Pack-bound gateway. It must not be retained and does not exist just
        because graph/plan records mention a pack.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Replace PackContext with ModCallContext or more precise activation-time
        invocation context terminology.

        Safer wording:

            ActivationPlan does not create ModCallContext authority, retained Pack
            gateway authority, adapter authority, capability permission, or
            execution authorization.

        Activation entries may later be used by activation workflows to decide
        what gets activated, but entries do not themselves create invocation
        contexts or authority.

4. onLoad(ctx) wording may preserve old activation callback shape.

    IS-11 says ActivationPlan does not call onLoad(ctx). If DA-14 has been or will
    be updated away from old PackContext/onLoad wording, this final IS should not
    fossilize the old callback signature as current activation API.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Keep the stop-line that ActivationPlan is not activation and does not
        invoke activation callbacks, but phrase callback examples through the
        accepted DA-14 vocabulary.

        Safe wording:

            ActivationPlan does not invoke Pack activation callbacks, construct
            ModCallContext, load executable Pack code, or call adapter execution
            entry points.

5. Pack-kind and SaveBundle interaction must align with IS-09.

    IS-11 selectedPacks are selected pack entries, but SaveBundle is not a Pack.
    SaveBundle graph references are persistence evidence, not selected pack
    entries.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Preserve selectedPacks as Pack entries only.

        SaveBundle graph evidence should reference copied/pinned Pack material
        retained by a SaveBundle without making the SaveBundle itself a selected
        Pack entry.

6. GraphPurpose names should be updated to SaveBundle terminology.

    NewSaveCreation, ExistingSaveLoad, SaveCompatibilityCheck, SaveRepair, and
    SaveExport are mostly generic enough, but their descriptions currently refer
    to SavePack.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Either keep the user-facing generic "Save" purpose names and update their
        descriptions to SaveBundle, or rename the purpose values if schema
        vocabulary is not yet locked.

7. Accepted override scope vocabulary includes save.

    decisionScope records run, save, global, graph, test, or another defined
    scope.

    This may be acceptable as user-facing generic "save" scope, but if it is a
    schema field tied to durable persistence identity, it should align with
    SaveBundle.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Clarify whether save means user-facing save scope or SaveBundle scope. If
        schema identity is meant, prefer SaveBundle scope terminology.

8. Valid / Ready / Prepared statuses need to remain non-execution.

    IS-11 already says Valid does not mean packs will activate successfully and
    Ready does not guarantee activation success.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Preserve this strongly. Prepared, Valid, Ready, and activation order are
        preparation evidence only, not activation success, adapter success, Job
        success, ApplicationRun readiness, capability availability, or execution
        authorization.

9. Source evidence and path exposure need file-access guardrails.

    IS-11 says sourcePath records a path only when path exposure is permitted and
    source evidence does not prove authenticity or grant authority.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Add or preserve wording that sourcePath is evidence only. It does not
        grant filesystem access, prove current file existence, or authorize direct
        reads.

10. Stored graph evidence must not become silent fallback.

    IS-11 already says stored graph evidence does not prove current material
    exists and does not bypass fresh dependency resolution where DA-13 requires
    it.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Preserve this and translate SavePack to SaveBundle.

11. Activation order groups must not imply concurrency.

    IS-11 already says groups do not imply concurrent activation unless a future
    authoritative concurrency mode defines that behavior.

    Required consequence for IS-11:
        Preserve this. Do not allow group structure to become scheduling,
        parallelism, adapter lifecycle, or OrchestrationUnit execution authority.

12. Extension fields must remain non-semantic.

    IS-11 reserves extensions for implementation-defined fields that do not
    redefine the schema.

    Required consequence:
        Preserve this. Extension fields must not redefine graph identity,
        activation plan identity, dependency semantics, activation ordering,
        persistence authority, or accepted override meaning.
