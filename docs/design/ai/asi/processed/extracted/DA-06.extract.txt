- Configuration persistence routes through IoManager and does not perform filesystem I/O directly.
- Migration is deterministic, ordered, state-authority-respecting, and aborts safely if it cannot complete.
- Recovery is explicit and trace-visible; no silent fallback is allowed.
- Development diagnostics may report config issues but do not rewrite stored configuration.
