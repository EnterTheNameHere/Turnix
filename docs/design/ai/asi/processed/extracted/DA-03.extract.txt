SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Tracing answers what happened, what caused what, and why.
- Tracing is structured evidence with explicit reasons and causal links.
- Tracing is not logging; plain logs are secondary and optional.
- Retained trace evidence is authoritative only for causality, decisions, audit reconstruction, debugger/tooling correlation, and policy-enforcement provenance represented inside retained trace evidence.
- Trace evidence is not authoritative for committed state, restoration, replay, rollback eligibility, or lifecycle truth outside retained trace evidence.
- "If it is not traced, it did not happen" means the action is not provable from retained trace evidence, not that DA-03 owns every domain tracing obligation.
- Trace evidence can represent successful, failed, refused, cancelled, superseded, and other domain-defined outcomes.
- A trace consumer does not infer an unrecorded outcome merely because failure evidence is absent.
- Subject-owning documents decide whether and how their objects, workflows, lifecycles, and subsystems use the trace substrate.
- Events form a trace DAG, not a flat stream.
- Causality is represented by explicit DAG links; Turnix does not infer causality from timestamps.
- Temporal ordering is observational only and does not determine causality or correctness.
- A trace event may have zero, one, or multiple parent events.
- Trace spans represent bounded work with a begin event, end event, stable span identity attribute, and explicit causality links.
- A span end does not exist without a reachable span begin.
- A snapshot or export that includes span end also includes the corresponding span begin.
- Trace storage is best-effort, non-blocking, bounded or rotating, and safe to drop under pressure.
- Trace loss is acceptable only when known loss or incompleteness is represented explicitly.
- Controlled trace access surfaces preserve causal meaning, do not fabricate reasons, and do not hide known evidence loss.
- User-facing views may simplify presentation but preserve causality and do not fabricate reasons.
- Error envelopes do not replace trace evidence.
- Development-mode trace enrichment is explicit, non-authoritative, and does not change runtime behavior.
- Domain-specific trace obligations are migrating to the documents that own the affected domains.
