SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Tracing answers what happened, what caused what, and why.
- Tracing is structured evidence with explicit reasons and causal links, not logging.
- Plain logs are secondary and optional; they do not replace trace evidence.
- Retained trace evidence is authoritative for retained causal proof, represented decisions, audit reconstruction, debugger/tooling correlation, and policy-enforcement provenance.
- Trace evidence is not authoritative committed state and does not define rollback eligibility by itself.
- Trace causality is represented through explicit DAG links, not inferred from timestamps.
- Temporal ordering is observational only.
- If an action is absent from retained trace evidence, the action is not provable from trace.
- Absence of failure evidence is not proof of success.
- Trace loss is acceptable only when known loss or incompleteness is explicitly represented.
- Trace consumers rely on controlled structured access surfaces, not direct file scraping.
- User-facing diagnostic views may simplify presentation but must preserve causality and must not fabricate reasons.
- Development-mode trace enrichment is explicit, non-authoritative, and does not change runtime behavior.
- Domain-specific trace obligations belong to the documents that own those domains.
- ODA-B diagnostics consume DA-03 evidence; they do not redefine DA-03 trace/evidence substrate semantics.
- ODA-B tooling may present trace-domain filters, but DA-03 owns trace-domain field mechanics and subject-owning documents own domain-specific trace obligations.
