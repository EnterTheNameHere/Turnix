SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Visibility answers only whether a requesting pack is permitted to reference a target pack.
- Visibility is permission, not requirement; dependency is requirement, not permission.
- A declared dependency can still fail because visibility forbids the target.
- Visibility violations are hard failures and cannot be silently bypassed.
- Local visibility expresses local manifest intent; globalVisibility is derived and controls reachability from outside the parent chain.
- exportNestedPacks gates only direct child exports and is not transitive.
- importPacksFromParent imports parent-side visibility downward; it does not export the child outward.
- importPacksFromParent list selectors resolve relative to the parent pack and cannot escape the parent tree.
- ViewPack defaults to importPacksFromParent=false to keep UI contamination explicit.
- Visibility evaluation happens after resolution-source determination; visibility does not bridge global and save-scoped sources.
- SavePack is never globally visible and copied packs inside a SavePack shadow global packs only within that save domain.
- Capabilities and services are separate from visibility and require their own mechanisms.
- Visibility is dynamically evaluated between requester and target during dependency resolution, resolvePack, and turnixImport resolution.
- Identifier comparisons for visibility are case-sensitive and byte-exact with no normalization.

CONFUSABLE_TERMS
----------------
- TERM: visibility vs dependency
  DISTINCTION: Visibility is permission/reachability; dependency is requirement. Dependency does not override visibility.
- TERM: visibility vs activation
  DISTINCTION: Visibility never activates packs, loads code, or implies execution order.
- TERM: visibility vs capability access
  DISTINCTION: Visibility does not grant capabilities; capability access requires explicit request and grant.
- TERM: visibility vs service access
  DISTINCTION: Visibility does not grant service access; services require activation-time registration and explicit lookup.
- TERM: declared visibility vs globalVisibility
  DISTINCTION: Declared visibility is local intent; globalVisibility is computed from parent/export conditions.
- TERM: exportNestedPacks vs importPacksFromParent
  DISTINCTION: exportNestedPacks controls child export upward/outward; importPacksFromParent controls parent-side visibility downward into a child.
- TERM: global pack vs save-scoped copied pack
  DISTINCTION: Visibility never bridges resolution sources; save-scoped copies shadow global packs only inside the save domain.
- TERM: public visibility vs visible candidate
  DISTINCTION: Public/global visibility does not make a pack visible across an inactive or different resolution source.
- TERM: ViewPack isolation vs app behavior leakage
  DISTINCTION: ViewPack defaults to importPacksFromParent=false; other pack kinds default to true.
