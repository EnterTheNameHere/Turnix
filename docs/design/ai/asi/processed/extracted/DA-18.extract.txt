--------------
DA-18 defines Turnix debugging, inspection, and breakpoint behavior. It covers
semantic breakpoints, pause behavior, live inspection, controlled debugger
mutation, manual pause, apply-at-safe-point mutation, undo and redo boundaries,
Job-aware debugging behavior, debugger user-interface integration, failure
modes, and debugger-specific trace obligations.
