SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Services and Capabilities are orthogonal mechanisms.
- Services provide shared runtime wiring; Capabilities provide named permissioned operations.
- Neither Services nor Capabilities implies persistence.
- Same process, same runtime, and same language do not bypass capability mediation.
- Service access is explicit, not inherited, not granted by visibility, and never implicit.
- Services are trusted runtime wiring only and must not bypass persistence, configuration, authorization, permission, adapter, capability, or Job boundaries.
- Capability export is runtime behavior through `ctx.registerCapability`; manifest declarations are optional and advisory only.
- Runtime behavior remains authoritative over manifest intent.
- Permission classification is capability-defined and immutable per major version.
- Permission evaluation occurs before capability execution begins.
- Undecided permission handling pauses execution, remains responsive, forbids background mutation while paused, and resumes deterministically from the pause point.
- Direct execution completes in the immediate invocation path; Job-backed execution is represented by or attached to a DA-19 Job lifecycle.
- Job-backed capability acceptance is not automatically final Job success.
- Computed/reactive behavior remains runtime-only and does not mutate stored configuration or memory by itself.
- Silent capability override is forbidden.
- Capability calls remain trace-visible, auditable, and causally linkable to Job and adapter traces where applicable.
