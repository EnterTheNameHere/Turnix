SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Diagnostic tooling consumes authority evidence; it does not become authority by presenting that evidence.
- Meaningful decisions remain explainable through trace-backed evidence where available.
- Tooling distinguishes missing evidence from evidence of absence.
- Tooling treats trace evidence as potentially incomplete.
- When evidence is incomplete because of pruning, dropping, corruption, or partial export, tooling surfaces that incompleteness explicitly and does not guess.
- ODA-B does not define the trace model; DA-03 defines the trace model.
- ODA-B tooling does not fabricate trace reasons or causal links.
- Trace-domain filters shown by ODA-B tooling are presentation aids only.
- ODA-B tooling does not invent trace-domain authority by showing a domain filter.
- Domain-specific trace reason ownership remains with the owning document.
- Development-mode enhancements do not change runtime correctness semantics.
- Development-mode diagnostics do not weaken tracing requirements defined elsewhere.
- Runtime safety warnings are not correctness errors unless a higher document classifies the situation otherwise.
- Transport timeout, adapter failure, Job timeout, and AppInstance failure are not interchangeable diagnostic categories.
- Adapter loading or readiness is not activation unless activation evidence supports that conclusion.
- Adapter-originated capability reports do not imply adapter-owned capability authority.
- Snapshot tooling distinguishes authoritative state from non-authoritative evidence.
