  View, and Job correlation metadata.
- Owns the stated model-location metadata for backend/rpc/models.py,
  frontend/assets/types.d.ts, and RpcClient helper utilities.

SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- IS-01 defines concrete wire structure only; higher DA documents own system
  guarantees, authority semantics, lifecycle semantics, policy, and permission.
- RPCMessage validity and completion are checked before routing, but validity
  does not imply successful work or semantic correctness.
- isComplete marks intentional construction of one complete top-level RPC
  envelope and is serialized as the final top-level field.
- isComplete is not operation completion, request success, work completion, Job
  success, semantic correctness, adapter readiness, AppInstance readiness, or
  committed-state mutation.
- final marks the final item in a streamed or ordered series; it is distinct
  from isComplete.
- stateUpdate is a concrete RPC message type and payload expectation; it does
  not by itself define committed authoritative state mutation.
- ack and ackOf are receipt or transport-level acknowledgement material only;
  they do not prove Job success.
- job metadata carried by the envelope is not the owner of Job lifecycle
  semantics.
- route, lane, op, path, idempotencyKey, seq, and origin help route, order,
  deduplicate, or correlate messages; they do not grant authority.
- origin metadata is correlation evidence only and does not grant permission,
  activation, adapter execution, lifecycle authority, or state mutation.
- seq orders messages within a lane; it is not a committed-state revision.
- The concrete envelope can carry trace/runtime correlation metadata even when
  the semantic meaning of that metadata belongs to subject-owner documents.

CONFUSABLE_TERMS
----------------
- TERM_A: isComplete
  TERM_B: final
  DISTINCTION: isComplete marks intentional completion of one top-level RPC
    envelope; final marks the final item in a streamed or ordered message
    series.

- TERM_A: isComplete
  TERM_B: operation completion / request success / work completion / Job success
  DISTINCTION: isComplete proves neither correctness nor successful work; it
    only marks complete envelope construction after parsing and validation.

- TERM_A: stateUpdate
  TERM_B: committed authoritative state mutation
  DISTINCTION: stateUpdate is a concrete RPC message type carrying update
    material; it does not by itself define or prove committed-state mutation.

- TERM_A: budgetMs
  TERM_B: Job timeout
  DISTINCTION: budgetMs is an envelope field; it does not define Job timeout
    unless a higher-level operation maps it to Job semantics.

- TERM_A: ack / ackOf / acknowledgement metadata
  TERM_B: Job success
  DISTINCTION: acknowledgement confirms receipt or transport-level handling
    only; Job lifecycle and outcome belong to DA-19.

- TERM_A: route / lane
  TERM_B: permission or execution authority
  DISTINCTION: route and lane support delivery and selection; permission and
    authority are defined by higher DA documents.

- TERM_A: origin metadata
  TERM_B: authority grant
  DISTINCTION: origin carries opaque correlation evidence and does not grant
    permission, activation, adapter execution, or state mutation.

- TERM_A: seq
  TERM_B: committed-state revision
  DISTINCTION: seq orders messages within a lane; it is not a committed-state
    revision.

- TERM_A: id
  TERM_B: appInstanceId / jobId / viewId / adapter identity
  DISTINCTION: id identifies the RPC message only; other identifiers are
    correlation metadata owned by their subject documents.

- TERM_A: payload validity
  TERM_B: payload authority
  DISTINCTION: Payload content does not gain authority merely because the
    envelope is valid.

  schema, field, message type, envelope, helper-constructor, or serialization
  behavior is involved.

  AppInstance, View, adapter, or trace semantics without envelope-field
  questions.
