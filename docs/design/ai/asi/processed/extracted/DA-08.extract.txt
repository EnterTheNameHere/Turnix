SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- A Window is a bounded region of runtime history and retained evidence associated with one AppInstance.
- Windows distinguish authoritative committed boundaries, non-authoritative in-flight activity, and non-authoritative historical evidence.
- Historical evidence may support explanation, audit, and debugging, but it does not become a hidden correctness dependency.
- Journal content is append-oriented, non-authoritative, non-blocking, and evidence-oriented.
- Journal data is not state and is not required for correctness.
- Journal retention loss does not change committed-state meaning.
- An AppInstance maintains one explicit ordered Window sequence.
- Every Window is exactly one of SafePointWindow, AbortedWindow, or ActiveWindow unless a later authoritative revision adds another kind.
- At most one ActiveWindow exists for one AppInstance at a time.
- ActiveWindow is the unique mutable tail of the Window sequence.
- SafePointWindow and AbortedWindow are finalized Window forms; ActiveWindow is not finalized.
- Window ordering is explicit and does not rely on timestamps, inference, or presentation order.
- SafePointWindow is a finalized authoritative committed boundary.
- SafePointWindow authoritative material contains committed layers and committed config only.
- Transactional layer contents are not authoritative SafePointWindow material.
- ActiveWindow is the current non-authoritative in-flight region after the last finalized committed boundary.
- ActiveWindow may be discarded during recovery and is not a deterministic replay boundary.
- AbortedWindow is finalized evidence of a non-commit outcome, not a committed boundary.
- AbortedWindow finalization does not add authority to retained evidence.
- ActiveWindow finalization produces exactly one outcome: SafePointWindow or AbortedWindow.
- Finalization never creates a partial finalized artifact or unresolved mixed state.
- Job terminal outcome, Orchestration Unit terminal outcome, and transaction commit/abort do not by themselves define Window finalization.
- SafePointWindow creation request modes are explicit and trace-visible, with no silent substitution.
- ImmediateCommitted attempts creation without waiting for quiescence and fails explicitly when immediate creation is not eligible.
- AtQuiescence attempts creation only when no Orchestration Unit is active for the target AppInstance.
- Turnix does not silently wait forever, cancel in-flight work, or downgrade AtQuiescence into ImmediateCommitted.
- SafePointWindow creation timeout is not Job timeout.
- SafePointWindow creation cancellation is not Job cancellation.
- Finalized Windows correlate to trace evidence through explicit retained trace identity and causal relationships rather than timestamps alone.
- Artifact and workflow documents consume Window finalization semantics and do not redefine them.
