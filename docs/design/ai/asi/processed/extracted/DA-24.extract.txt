Semantic anchors
----------------
- Orchestration Unit: kernel-tracked execution unit that may be permitted to mutate Turnix-managed state within an AppInstance.
- Orchestration Unit authority is scoped, not general backend authority.
- Work started outside Turnix orchestration remains outside Turnix guarantees until it re-enters through orchestration boundaries.
- Mutation outside orchestration does not acquire Turnix guarantees by implication.
- Mutation authority remains bounded by AppInstance identity, capability and permission boundaries, transactions and commits, adapter boundaries, RuntimeHost boundaries, and policy/decision boundaries.
- Staged mutation inside an Orchestration Unit is not authoritative merely because the unit exists.
- Staged mutation becomes authoritative only by commit under DA-04.
- Orchestration Unit outcome and transaction commit/abort are distinct.
- Orchestration Unit outcome and Job terminal outcome are distinct.
- Orchestration Unit outcome and Window finalization are distinct.
- RuntimeHost operation outcome does not by itself define Orchestration Unit terminal outcome unless explicitly mapped by the owning workflow.
- Adapter-executed mutation seeking Turnix guarantees re-enters through orchestration, capability, transaction, and commit boundaries.
- RuntimeHost-operation mutation seeking Turnix guarantees re-enters through orchestration, capability, transaction, policy, persistence, and commit boundaries.
- Within one AppInstance, Turnix does not execute multiple Orchestration Units concurrently when they may mutate the same AppInstance state unless an explicit concurrency mode permits it.
- Suspension during awaited I/O, model calls, adapter calls, RuntimeHost calls, or transport calls does not authorize concurrent state-mutating units in the same AppInstance.
