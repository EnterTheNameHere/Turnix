SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- ComparisonResult is a semantic claim scoped by an explicit equality mode.
- ComparisonResult has exactly three values: Equal, NotEqual, NotComparable.
- Convenience boolean equality APIs are not provided for finalized artifacts.
- NotComparable is distinct from NotEqual and Equal.
- Tools do not silently retry with a weaker equality mode and present the weaker result as if it answered the original request.
- StateEquality compares retained committed key/value pairs only and ignores evidence and revision history.
- StrictStateEquality compares committed key/value pairs plus required retained revision history.
- EvidenceEquality compares retained evidence closure as included and does not imply state equality.
- ExportEquality compares exported closure as packaged and does not by itself imply StateEquality or EvidenceEquality.
- Optional digests accelerate comparison but are not identities and do not add authority.
- Replay restores committed state within the DA-04 restoration boundary and may present retained causal evidence.
- Replay does not imply re-running external effects or resuming retained runtime identities.
- Resume begins new work from a restored committed boundary and is not deterministic in outcome.
- Retry is new work and does not reuse the previous Job, Orchestration Unit, or adapter execution identity.
- Audit workflows do not mutate state, require journal completeness, or assume replayability.
- Missing material, pruning, corruption, partial export, unsupported scope, and retained gaps remain explicit.

CONFUSABLE_TERMS
----------------
- TERM: Equal vs NotComparable
  DISTINCTION: Equal means the selected mode is satisfied; NotComparable means required material or support for that selected mode is missing, unavailable, unsupported, or unusable.
- TERM: NotEqual vs NotComparable
  DISTINCTION: NotComparable is not treated as NotEqual and must be presented separately.
- TERM: ComparisonResult vs boolean equality
  DISTINCTION: DA-26 forbids convenience boolean equality APIs for finalized artifacts.
- TERM: StateEquality vs StrictStateEquality
  DISTINCTION: StateEquality compares committed key/value pairs only; StrictStateEquality also requires retained revision history for the strict mode.
- TERM: EvidenceEquality vs StateEquality
  DISTINCTION: EvidenceEquality compares evidence closure and does not imply state equality unless state modes are also satisfied.
- TERM: ExportEquality vs EvidenceEquality
  DISTINCTION: ExportEquality compares packaged export closure; EvidenceEquality compares retained evidence closure as included.
- TERM: digest vs identity
  DISTINCTION: Optional digest fields may accelerate comparison but are not identities and do not add authority.
- TERM: Replay vs Resume
  DISTINCTION: Replay restores retained committed state and available causal story; Resume attempts new continuation after restoration.
- TERM: Resume vs Retry
  DISTINCTION: Resume may choose retry by policy, but Retry is a new orchestration attempt for selected prior work.
- TERM: Retry vs previous execution
  DISTINCTION: Retry may reference earlier evidence but is not part of the earlier execution and does not reuse prior lifecycle identity.
- TERM: audit output vs committed state
  DISTINCTION: Audit output remains evidence presentation unless a higher authority defines a committed-state mutation path.
