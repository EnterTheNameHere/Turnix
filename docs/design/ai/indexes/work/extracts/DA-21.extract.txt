PRIMARY_DOMAINS
---------------
- Abstract RPC communication semantics.
- Transport-neutral channel boundaries.
- Logical request, reply, emit, subscribe, cancel, error, update, and
  acknowledgement flow.
- Distinction between message delivery, message acknowledgement, request
  acceptance, handler execution, Job creation, Job lifecycle, adapter lifecycle,
  RuntimeHost operation lifecycle, and final work outcome.
- Routing boundaries for object, capability, adapter-facing, control, and
  subscription messages.
- Transport timeout, disconnect, and cancellation semantics at the RPC layer.
- RPC correlation and trace-observability obligations.

SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- RPC is a logical boundary, not WebSocket, JSON, a transport, capability
  authority, adapter authority, RuntimeHost authority, or Job lifecycle.
- Transport carries RPC messages but does not define RPC semantics.
- RPCChannel is the transport-neutral surface consumed by the RPC host or
  equivalent runtime owner.
- Message delivery, acknowledgement, request acceptance, handler execution, Job
  creation, and Job terminal outcome are distinct events.
- Acknowledgement means receipt or transport/RPC handling only, not request
  success, capability execution, adapter execution, RuntimeHost operation
  completion, Job creation, or Job success.
- Request acceptance is distinct from final operation success unless an
  operation contract explicitly defines acceptance as the result.
- Routing identifies a logical target but does not grant permission, activate
  packs, select adapters, or bypass capability mediation.
- Transport timeout and disconnect are RPC/channel/transport concerns, not Job,
  adapter, or RuntimeHost terminal outcomes by themselves.
- RPC cancellation is a request, not a direct execution kill.
- Subscription delivery may carry progress or state, but the meaning of Job,
  adapter, or RuntimeHost evidence remains owned by the corresponding domain.
- RPC trace evidence preserves correlation between messages and higher-domain
  events where those events exist.

CONFUSABLE_TERMS
----------------
- TERM_A: RPC
  TERM_B: WebSocket
  DISTINCTION: RPC is the logical communication boundary; WebSocket is one
    concrete transport whose flow belongs to IS-02.

- TERM_A: RPC
  TERM_B: JSON envelope
  DISTINCTION: DA-21 owns abstract RPC semantics; concrete JSON envelope fields
    belong to IS-01.

- TERM_A: RPCChannel
  TERM_B: transport
  DISTINCTION: RPCChannel is the transport-neutral interface; transport is the
    concrete carrier.

- TERM_A: transport adapter
  TERM_B: Execution Adapter
  DISTINCTION: A transport adapter adapts carrier events into RPCChannel events;
    Execution Adapter semantics belong to DA-23.

- TERM_A: acknowledgement
  TERM_B: request success
  DISTINCTION: Acknowledgement confirms receipt or transport/RPC handling only;
    request success depends on the operation contract.

- TERM_A: acknowledgement
  TERM_B: Job success
  DISTINCTION: Acknowledgement does not imply Job creation or Job terminal
    success.

- TERM_A: acknowledgement
  TERM_B: RuntimeHost operation completion
  DISTINCTION: Acknowledgement does not imply RuntimeHost command acceptance or
    operation completion.

- TERM_A: request acceptance
  TERM_B: final operation success
  DISTINCTION: Acceptance means the request crossed the dispatch boundary and
    was accepted by the target operation; final success is separate unless the
    operation contract defines otherwise.

- TERM_A: transport timeout
  TERM_B: Job timeout
  DISTINCTION: Transport timeout is an RPC/channel/transport concern; Job
    timeout is a Job lifecycle concern owned by DA-19.

- TERM_A: transport disconnect
  TERM_B: Job cancellation
  DISTINCTION: Disconnect does not automatically cancel, supersede, discard, or
    fail Job-backed work.

- TERM_A: transport disconnect
  TERM_B: RuntimeHost failure
  DISTINCTION: Disconnect does not automatically stop, fail, or close a
    RuntimeHost; RuntimeHost semantics belong to DA-28.

- TERM_A: unsubscribe
  TERM_B: cancellation of underlying work
  DISTINCTION: Unsubscribe withdraws delivery interest; it cancels underlying
    work only if operation contract or policy explicitly connects them.
