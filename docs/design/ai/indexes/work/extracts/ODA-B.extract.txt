SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Diagnostic tooling consumes authority evidence; it does not become authority by presenting it.
- Meaningful decisions remain explainable through trace-backed evidence when available.
- Missing evidence is not the same as evidence that something did not happen.
- Tooling surfaces incompleteness explicitly instead of guessing.
- ODA-B presents and correlates retained evidence but does not fabricate reasons, causal links, or authority.
- Development-mode diagnostics may be more verbose or stricter, but do not change runtime correctness semantics.
- Diagnostic categories such as transport timeout, adapter failure, Job timeout, and AppInstance failure are not interchangeable.
- Trace domain filters are presentation aids and do not create trace-domain authority.
- ODA-B ERROR/WARNING/INFO severity vocabulary is not automatically the same taxonomy as DA-03 trace event level.
