SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Diagnostic tooling consumes authority evidence; it does not become authority by presenting it.
- Meaningful decisions remain explainable through trace-backed evidence when available.
- Missing evidence is not the same as evidence that something did not happen.
- Tooling surfaces incompleteness explicitly instead of guessing.
- ODA-B presents and correlates retained evidence but does not fabricate reasons, causal links, or authority.
- Development-mode diagnostics may be more verbose or stricter, but do not change runtime correctness semantics.
- Diagnostic categories such as transport timeout, adapter failure, Job timeout, and AppInstance failure are not interchangeable.
- Trace domain filters are presentation aids and do not create trace-domain authority.

  DISTINCTION: ODA-B tooling reports and presents evidence; it does not create new correctness guarantees.

- TERM_A: missing evidence
  TERM_B: evidence of absence
  DISTINCTION: Missing evidence means the tool cannot prove the fact; evidence of absence means retained evidence supports that the thing did not occur.

- TERM_A: trace viewer domain filter
  TERM_B: trace-domain authority
  DISTINCTION: Showing a filter does not make ODA-B the authority for that domain or reason vocabulary.

- TERM_A: adapter loading or readiness
  TERM_B: activation
  DISTINCTION: Tooling must not present adapter readiness as activation unless activation evidence supports that conclusion.

- TERM_A: adapter-originated report
  TERM_B: adapter-owned authority
  DISTINCTION: A report produced from adapter-originated evidence does not imply the adapter owns capability authority.

- TERM_A: development-mode diagnostics
  TERM_B: changed runtime semantics
  DISTINCTION: Development mode may increase verbosity or strictness of diagnostics but does not alter correctness semantics.

- TERM_A: snapshot tooling
  TERM_B: authoritative state
  DISTINCTION: Snapshots may contain evidence and state views, but snapshot tooling does not create authority semantics by itself.

- TERM_A: transport timeout
  TERM_B: adapter failure
  DISTINCTION: ODA-B requires tooling not to collapse transport, adapter, Job, and AppInstance failures into one interchangeable category.
