SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- Diagnostic tooling consumes authority evidence; it does not become authority by presenting that evidence.
- Meaningful decisions remain explainable through trace-backed evidence where available.
- Missing evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Tooling surfaces incompleteness explicitly and does not guess when evidence is incomplete.
- ODA-B tooling does not fabricate trace reasons, causal links, or authority.
- Trace-domain filters are presentation aids and do not create canonical trace-domain authority.
- DA-03 owns trace field mechanics and trace event levels.
- Subject-owning documents own domain-specific trace obligations.
- ODA-B diagnostics are optional developer-facing tooling expectations that consume evidence.
- DA-03 diagnostics own trace/evidence substrate constraints and user-facing diagnostic causality constraints.
- ODA-B ERROR/WARNING/INFO severity vocabulary is not automatically the same taxonomy as DA-03 trace event level.
- Development-mode diagnostics may be more verbose or stricter but do not change runtime correctness semantics.
- Transport timeout, adapter failure, Job timeout, and AppInstance failure are not interchangeable diagnostic categories.
- Adapter loading or readiness is not activation unless activation evidence supports that conclusion.
- Adapter-originated capability reports do not imply adapter-owned capability authority.
- Snapshot tooling distinguishes authoritative state from non-authoritative evidence.
