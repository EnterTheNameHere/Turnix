PRIMARY_DOMAINS
---------------
- Concrete RPC wire-envelope schema.
- RPCMessage, Gen, and Route wire models.
- RPC envelope fields and concrete message type values.
- Helper-constructor expectations for validated envelope construction.
- Canonical serialization order and envelope completion-marker behavior.
- Receiver-side incomplete-envelope rejection before routing.
- Correlation metadata carriage for trace, runtime, adapter, View, AppInstance,
  and Job context.

OWNERSHIP_HINTS
---------------
- Owns the concrete RPCMessage envelope shape used between runtime participants.
- Owns Gen, Route, and RPCMessage as core wire models.
- Owns the meaning of top-level RPC envelope fields.
- Owns concrete type values: hello, welcome, clientReady, heartbeat, ack,
  request, emit, reply, subscribe, stateUpdate, unsubscribe, cancel, error.
- Owns per-message-type field expectations, including route, payload,
  correlatesTo, error payload, state-update payload, and isComplete carriage.
- Owns helper-constructor expectations for required fields, correlation copying,
  lane inference, idempotency handling, and completion marking.
- Owns isComplete as the final serialized top-level envelope field and tail
  sentinel for complete envelopes.
- Owns schema-level handling of malformed or incomplete envelopes before
  routing.
- Owns schema-level carriage of adapter, AppInstance, View, trace, and Job
  correlation metadata.

NON_OWNERSHIP_HINTS

- Does not make valid serialization proof of successful work, semantic

  mutation.

SEMANTIC_ANCHORS
----------------
- IS-01 defines concrete wire structure, not new authority semantics.
- Envelope validity and completion are checked before routing, but they do not
  imply semantic correctness or successful work.
- isComplete marks intentional completion of one top-level RPC envelope; it is
  not operation completion, request success, Job success, work completion, or
  committed-state mutation.
- final marks the final item in a streamed or ordered series; it is not the same
  as isComplete.
- stateUpdate is a concrete message type and payload expectation; it does not by
  itself define committed authoritative state mutation.
- ack and acknowledgement metadata confirm receipt or transport-level handling
  only, not Job success.
- job metadata carried by an envelope is not the owner of Job lifecycle
  semantics.
- route, lane, op, path, idempotencyKey, and origin help route or correlate
  messages; they do not grant permission or execution authority.
- origin metadata is correlation evidence only.
- seq orders messages within a lane; it is not a committed-state revision.
- Trace and runtime correlation metadata in origin do not define trace,
  AppInstance, View, adapter, or Job lifecycle semantics.

CONFUSABLE_TERMS
----------------
- TERM_A: isComplete
  TERM_B: final
  DISTINCTION: isComplete marks intentional completion of one top-level RPC
    envelope; final marks the final item in a streamed or ordered message
    series.

- TERM_A: isComplete
  TERM_B: operation completion / request success / work completion / Job success
  DISTINCTION: isComplete proves neither correctness nor successful work; it
    only marks complete envelope construction after parsing and validation.

- TERM_A: stateUpdate
  TERM_B: committed authoritative state mutation
  DISTINCTION: stateUpdate is a concrete RPC message type carrying update
    material; it does not by itself define or prove committed-state mutation.

- TERM_A: budgetMs
  TERM_B: Job timeout
  DISTINCTION: budgetMs is an envelope field; it does not define Job timeout
    unless a higher-level operation maps it to Job semantics.

- TERM_A: ack / acknowledgement metadata
  TERM_B: Job success
  DISTINCTION: acknowledgement confirms receipt or transport-level handling
    only; Job lifecycle and outcome belong to DA-19.

- TERM_A: route / lane
  TERM_B: permission or execution authority
  DISTINCTION: route and lane support delivery and selection; permission and
    authority are defined by higher DA documents.

- TERM_A: origin metadata
  TERM_B: authority grant
  DISTINCTION: origin carries opaque correlation evidence and does not grant
    permission, activation, adapter execution, or state mutation.

- TERM_A: seq
  TERM_B: committed-state revision
  DISTINCTION: seq orders messages within a lane; it is not a committed-state
    revision.

- TERM_A: id
  TERM_B: appInstanceId / jobId / viewId / adapter identity
  DISTINCTION: id identifies the RPC message only; other identifiers are
    correlation metadata owned by their subject documents.

- TERM_A: payload validity
  TERM_B: payload authority
  DISTINCTION: Payload content does not gain authority merely because the
    envelope is valid.
